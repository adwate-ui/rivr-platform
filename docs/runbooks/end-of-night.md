# End of night

A club night ends at 06:00 IST. The system tidies up what staff leave open, but the floor and the front desk should
close the night themselves, so the numbers each player and table show are right.

## Before the last player leaves
1. **Floor: end every seat.** On Live floor, let each player still seated go (Leaves). A table cannot be closed while
   anyone sits at it.
2. **Floor: close every table.** That ends each table's run, which Reports, Tables reads.
3. **Floor: empty the waitlists.** Remove anyone still waiting on the Waitlist screen.
4. **Front desk: check out everyone still in the room.** Check-in's room list shows who has not checked out.
5. **Dealers: end your down** on the tablet before you leave it.

## What the system does if something is left open (B5 of the Fix Tracker)
- **A seat forgotten overnight.** A seat from an earlier night that staff, or the player from the app, end more than two
  hours after 06:00 is stamped at 06:00. It earns no hours past its night. A player who stayed just past 06:00 is
  stamped when they actually leave.
- **A visit nobody checked out.** A visit from an earlier night with nobody seated, and nothing after 06:00, is closed as
  end of day at its last seat's end. This happens when the member checks in, is seated or scans at the door, before
  tonight's visit opens. A member who played on past 06:00 keeps their visit until they leave. A seat still open keeps
  the visit open until the floor ends it.
- **Waiting places.** A waiting place from an earlier night is on no list, so nobody at home is called. It is cleared
  when that member's old visit closes. Tonight's places stay when a member steps out or is seated from one.
- **A dealer's down from an earlier night** is no one's down: the tablet asks for a dealer to sign in, which ends it.
