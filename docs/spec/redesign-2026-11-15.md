# Redesign, 15 Nov 2026

The owner's brief of 15 Nov 2026 for the player app, the ops console and the TV display, turned into slices with acceptance criteria. The brief itself is `docs/design/redesign-2026-11-15/brief.md`; the renders it refers to are `docs/design/redesign-2026-11-15/screens/` (named below without the folder or `.png`).

## How to read this

1. Order of authority: the brief, then the renders, then the app on `main`. **The existing app stands unless the brief changes it.** A render that shows fewer rows, fields, columns, states or steps than today's screen removes nothing.
2. Where a render disagrees with the brief, with itself or with today's app, "Corrections to the prototype" below says which reading is built.
3. Two lines of the brief are superseded by the repository's own rules: "English and Hindi" (English only, decision 0027) and "PRs under 400 changed lines, one issue per PR" (batches of three or four issues, one commit each, decision 0257). A removal is exempt from any line count: it deletes thousands of lines.
4. Owner rulings given on 15 Nov while this was written are marked "(owner, 15 Nov)".
5. Every slice keeps `CLAUDE.md`'s "Done means", plus: the screen matches its renders on the target device, nothing outside "Removals" disappears, loading, empty, error and offline states exist as today, and the practice venue behaves the same.

## Corrections to the prototype

A copy and polish pass over the 82 renders and the prototype's source (owner, 15 Nov). Each line is what the prototype shows, then what is built. The owner answered every open question on 15 Nov; the answers are in the lines they settle and listed under "Issues".

### Structure

1. **Three ways to change a record.** The prototype opens a right-hand panel for the member record, a promotion, a new tournament, new staff, a new order, the ID check and a high hand; it edits Tables setup and Settings inside the row; and it gives the promotion builder a page. Today's console is the other way round: most forms open inside the page (Tables, Settings, Staff, Notices, Feedback), four open the right-hand `Sheet` (Live floor, seating from the Waitlist, a new order, the ID check in person), Members docks a panel, and the builder is a page. **Built:** one rule. A form that creates or changes a record opens the right-hand `Sheet place="console"`; the promotion builder stays a page, as the brief says. Tables setup and Settings follow the rule. The rule reaches the screens the brief calls unchanged as well: Staff, Notices, Feedback and the menu (owner, 15 Nov; RD-38).
2. **The source holds two of several screens.** Tables as a list with a panel and as the setup page; "ID check at the desk" as one form and "ID check in person" as five steps; a promotion made in a panel and in the builder. **Built:** what the renders show, corrected by line 1.
3. **Row actions differ by screen.** Tables rows carry "Change" and a "..." menu, stakes only "Change", promotions one button that changes with the state, Door "Check out", Feedback "Resolve". **Built:** one visible button for the row's main action, everything else in the "..." menu (`OverflowMenu`, as Tables setup has today). A stake no table uses can be deleted from that menu.
4. **Two filters for one thing, with different counts.** Members has status chips above the table ("On a break 3") and a Status column menu ("On a break 1"); the menu counts only the twelve loaded rows. **Built:** a column menu's counts come from the server, over every row the other filters leave. The chips above stay where they exist today.
5. **"Clear all" with nothing to clear.** It appears when only a sort is on. **Built:** it shows only while a filter is on, beside the filters' chips, as today.
6. **Two counts on one screen, worded seven ways.** "4,812 members" by the search and "Showing 12 of 4,812" in the foot; elsewhere "36 players", "2 notes", "5 rows", "7 settings", "1,204 entries". **Built:** one count by the search, in today's words ("12 of 4,812"); the foot holds only the pages.
7. **Seats are a fixed row of chips, 2 to 10.** The brief's rule is that counts are typed. **Built:** the fixed row, as drawn (owner, 15 Nov): a table seats 2 to 10 and nothing else.
8. **A stake is finished in two places.** The stake form has no call time, yet a stake cannot be used without one, and Settings says "A new stake appears here when you add it in Tables setup". **Built:** the stake form asks for the call time; it is the same value the Settings row shows and changes.
9. **Four ways to type a day and time.** "DD/MM/YYYY" with a separate time (notices), one day field (builder, settings), and free text "Sun 29 Nov, 16:00" (tournament). **Built:** the day and time fields `@rivr/ui` already has, everywhere.
10. **A rule nobody confirms.** "4 or more players dealt in" is on the TV and in the promotion, but logging a hand confirms only the other two rules. **Built:** every rule the promotion holds is confirmed when a hand is logged.
11. **Hands are free text in one place and a fixed list in the other.** The builder takes "Any full house" as typed words; logging offers "Four of: Aces, Kings...". A hand can also sit in the list with no Bonus ("Aces full, Not paid"). **Built (owner, 15 Nov):** hands come from a fixed list in `@rivr/domain`; the admin adds and removes hands from that list and types each Bonus; a listed hand always has a Bonus.
12. **The Door still searches by card.** Its search reads "Card, name, mobile or member number". **Built:** "Name, mobile or member number".
13. **Players see how many wait and how many seats are open.** Home's table rows read "2 waiting" and "4 open", with a "Seats open" filter. The owner ruled on 8 Nov, and again on 15 Nov, that players see neither. **Built:** today's rows, with the minimum buy-in and no counts.
14. **Names that differ from the brief.** The rail says "Floor" and "Orders"; the brief says "Live floor" and "Food and drink". **Built:** the brief's names.
15. **Practice venue is a Settings tab, yet Practice reset is absent.** **Built:** Practice reset is removed (owner, 15 Nov). The practice venue itself stays.
16. **The TV's boards have two names each.** "Seats opening" for Call time, and "Most hours played" on a board the console lists as "Hours leaderboard". **Built:** "Seats opening" on the TV, "Call time" on the console and for the rule (owner, 15 Nov); the hours board keeps today's names.
17. **Lengths of time are written five ways.** "5 h 39 min", "5 h 39 m", "1 h 05 min", "3 h", "1 hour". **Built:** the one short form `@rivr/i18n` formats today.
18. **"Never expires" is written into the balance card.** Settings holds "Bonus expires". **Built:** the line follows the setting.
19. **The report's period control is a dashed pill** unlike any other control. **Built:** today's period control.
20. **Two notices can be live; Home has one slot.** **Built:** the slot shows the notice that started last. Today's limit of two at once goes.
21. **"Daily Bonus limit" reads as a limit on a member.** Today the limit is on what one staff member gives in a day. **Built:** today's limit stays, and a high hand payout is independent of it (owner, 15 Nov).

## Slices

### 0. Bonus gets a home of its own (RD-01)

Today the member's bonus balance, its history and refer a friend sit inside the player's Rewards tab, and staff can add bonus only from the Points screen. Both go in slice 1, so this comes first.

- The player app has a Bonus tab: balance, history, refer a friend. Pushes about bonus open it.

- Renders: `notice-rule-and-high-hand-payout-q03`, `add-time-and-bonus-by-hand-c02`.

### 1. Removals (RD-02 to RD-11)

One feature per PR, in this order. Each removes the screens, routes, feature keys, tier-gate rows, settings, text, seed data, TV boards, Edge Functions, outbox events, domain modules, golden files and tests, and drops its tables, columns, functions and jobs in a new migration once nothing reads them. Before a drop reaches production, the rows are counted there and the tables backed up.

- **Points and Rewards (RD-02).** Includes points on verification, the daily points limit, locked points, the points-multiplier reward and its templates, the hand-over tab on the Door, the website's points lines, and points inside visit close, erasure and the promotion engine. Tiers stay: staff set them, points never did.
- **RIVR League (RD-03).** Includes the season, finishes and adjustments, the CSV import, the website block and the member export's League rows.
- **Academy and Learn (RD-04).** Includes bookings and payments, the graduate tier (also for new venues), its templates, `packages/drills`, `docs/spec/learn`, and the Academy line in Events.
- **Membership card and QR code (RD-05).** The Card tab, the code's making and checking, the door kiosk on the tablet. The Door checks in by name, mobile or member number. Profile reads the tier, photo and member number from the member's own record, and shows the number, which only the card showed.
- **Spin wheel (RD-06).** The wheel, its prizes and budget, the free-spin reward and its template, the TV board.
- **Social (RD-07).** The wall, moderation, the TV board, publishing, leads, the inbox and winner cards: all of it (owner, 15 Nov).
- **Kill switch and Purge (RD-08).** The Kill switch screen is the only control that freezes or releases the room or stops messages. Owner, 15 Nov: remove it all, with nothing in its place; the checks behind a freeze stay in the database unused. Production must have nothing frozen or stopped when this ships. Member erasure shares helpers with Purge; those stay.
- **The older console screens (RD-09).** The 26 that show "Not used at RIVR", their routes, folders and text, except Tournaments.
- **Cage, markers, TDS and the other money tables (RD-10).** Dropped (owner, 15 Nov), with shift, accounts, withdrawals, expenses and Tally. The ledger stays: Bonus is paid through it.
- **What else only those screens used (RD-11).** Dealers and roster, jackpots and the old high hands, events, creators, shareholders, AML, pot data, certification, SOPs, incidents, the owner dashboard. ### 4. Stakes, call time per stake, the table's minimum buy-in (RD-16, RD-17)

Today the minimum buy-in belongs to the stake, is set by the database at 100 big blinds, and has no field anywhere; a new stake gets a call time of three hours without being asked.

- **Rule and migration (RD-16).** The minimum buy-in belongs to the table, in integer paise, required, with no maximum. Existing tables take their stake's value. A stake has a call time or cannot be given to a table; existing stakes keep theirs. Use the `money-rule` skill.
- **Console and player (RD-17).** Stakes as today (name, small blind, big blind, optional straddle) plus the call time (correction 8). A table has a name, seats (picked from 2 to 10), a stake and a minimum buy-in prefilled at 50 big blinds of the chosen stake and editable. Forms open the right-hand panel (correction 1). The player's rows show the table's own minimum buy-in. A stake with a straddle is written with it, "NLH 100/200/500", wherever a stake or a table's stake is named (owner, 15 Nov).
- Renders: `tables-setup-c01` to `c05`, `signup-app-kyc-and-notices-q07`.

### 5. Settings (RD-18, RD-19)

- **Regrouping (RD-18).** Floor: Seating, Time at the table, Identity. Members and Bonus: Bonus, Membership. TVs: Rotation. Every existing setting not tied to a removal stays, in the group that fits, with these rulings (owner, 15 Nov): TV hours leaderboard, Bonus per visit and request targets stay; hotel night per hours played, door code refresh, dealer rotation and the Google review link go, the link after a rating with it. In Club details, "SAC code, Academy" and "Cage and admin networks" go; nothing else there changes. Notices and Club details are untouched. A change applies from now or from a typed day and time, never backwards, as today.
- **Typed values (RD-19).** No fixed lists of values. Add time choices is a list of typed lengths, used by the player's Add time sheet however many there are. Call time is one typed value per stake. The call time when seating a player is typed, starting from the stake's value on both seat sheets (today they start differently). The amount of Bonus added by hand is typed and capped by its setting.
- Renders: `settings-c01` to `c06`, `signup-app-kyc-and-notices-c01` to `c03`, `add-time-and-bonus-by-hand-q01`, `login-and-club-details-06-settings-club-details`.

### 8. High hand bonus (RD-22 to RD-25)

- **Rule (RD-22).** A promotion holds hands, each with its Bonus, and rules: both hole cards must play, shown at showdown, minimum players dealt in. The floor logs a hit against a table, a seated player and a hand. The Bonus is paid at once, every time: no hourly window, no best hand. One idempotent ledger entry, within the promotion's budget and independent of the daily limit (correction 21). One live high hand promotion a venue. Use the `money-rule` skill.
- **Builder (RD-23).** "High hand" is a reward type in the builder, with its hands (correction 11), rules, the one-line summary, preview, simulation and approval as for any other type. Every existing reward type stays except points multiplier, free spin, tournament entry and cab home (owner, 15 Nov).
- **Live floor (RD-24).** A strip: hands paid tonight, Bonus paid, last hit, "Log a high hand". Logging opens the panel: table, seated player, hand, each rule confirmed (correction 10), then pay. Activity shows the hit and who logged it.
- **Member and TV (RD-25).** A carousel card with the hands and their Bonus; on payment the Bonus lands at once with a toast and a history line; the TV board updates in real time.
- Renders: `high-hand-and-promotions-c01` to `c09` and `t01`, `notice-rule-and-high-hand-payout-c02` and `q02` to `q03`, `tv-boards-05-high-hand-bonus`.

Design for several hundred orders, sign-ins and check-ins a day and a five-figure member list. Every list sorts, filters, searches and pages on the server, with indexes to match; none loads unbounded rows.
