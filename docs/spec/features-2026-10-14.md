# Features confirmed on 14 Oct 2026

Each item names its lane.

**Roles.** Exactly these, with permissions in one table and checked by row-level security: `super_admin` (owner level: all data, purge, exports, kill switch, configuration), `admin` (floor manager: active games, waitlist, call times, promotions, tournaments), `cashier`, `dealer`, `marketing`, `host`, `owner_view` (read-only dashboards). Lane 0.

**Tournaments** (lane B). Templates with blind structures; formats: freezeout, re-entry, rebuy and add-on, bounty and progressive bounty, satellite issuing tickets to a target event, guaranteed with overlay, multi-flight with day 2. Registration and re-entry from the app (wallet debit) or the cage; late registration to a set level; alternates list when full; random seating and balancing prompts; server-side clock that survives any device dropping; bust-out recording; prize pool, payouts and ICM or chip-chop deals; TDS on prizes through the TDS engine; League points on completion. Every rule in `domain` with golden tests.

**Check-in and check-out** (lane C door screen, lane A cage). Check-in by membership QR opens a visit; check-out at the door or on final cash-out closes it. The closed visit drives hours, points, cab eligibility, feedback and the third-visit review request.

**Call time** (lane B, cage side lane A). The minimum time a player must play after being seated before leaving the game. Configured per table type with effective dates (for example two hours in the Private Room). Seating starts the clock; the player app, the tablet seat ring and the console show time remaining through `CallTimeTimer`. A cash-out or check-out before call time is complete shows the cashier the shortfall and needs an admin override with a reason; every early departure is recorded against the member and the count is visible to admins. Promotions can use "completed call time" as a condition. Golden tests cover the clock across table moves, breaks and seat changes.

**Wallet** (lane A). Cash balance, bonus balance and points shown together in the app; points are a separate ledger and never convert to cash.

**Pot data** (lane B). Every hand from the tablet: table, dealer, variant, pot, rake, cap applied, jackpot drop, high-hand claim, timestamp, camera reference. Super admin views and exports by table, dealer, variant and period.

**Website editing** (lane C). Super admin and marketing edit the website from the console, GPA style: pages made of typed blocks from `@rivr/ui` (hero, text, image, gallery, games and stakes, tournament calendar, live floor, house rules, careers, FAQ, map, store), edited in place on a live preview rendered from the same `@rivr/ui` blocks, with drafts, scheduled publish, version history and one-click rollback. Publish purges the Cloudflare cache, so changes are live in seconds. Live blocks read public views; they are placed, not typed. No free-form drag-and-drop page builder.

**Social** (lane D). Instagram and WhatsApp as already specified: one-click publishing, winner cards with consent, inbox into leads, social wall with moderation.

**Kill switch** (lane A owns the control; every lane honours it). Five levels, each a row in `control_state`, checked inside the database functions that write, so a cached client cannot bypass it:
1. Venue freeze: every money movement, seating and hand write refused; all apps show a fixed banner; TVs show the club closed; reads continue.
2. Feature kill: switch off any of withdrawals, credit, KYC sign-up, marketing sends, social publishing, promotions, tournaments registration.
3. Messaging stop: all outbound WhatsApp and social posts halt and queued items hold.
4. Member freeze: one member cannot transact or join a waitlist.
5. Staff and device revoke: sessions end immediately and the device must re-enrol.
Any super admin can engage a level; release requires a reason, and venue freeze release requires a second super admin. Every change is audited and alerts the owner dashboard. The paper fallback SOP applies while a freeze is on.

**Exports** (lane A). From any data table in the console, export the filtered view as CSV or XLSX. Super admin full export: one encrypted archive of every table as CSV with a manifest and row counts, KYC images excluded unless selected with a written reason. Per-member export for access requests. Every export is audited with who, what, filter and row count, and large exports alert the owner.

**Purge** (lane A). A testing tool for `super_admin` only; no other role can see it, call it or be granted it, enforced in the database. The purge screen selects a scope: venue (including the practice venue), date range, and entity types (members, visits, sessions, hands, tournaments, ledger, promotions, media, messages), or everything. It shows a dry-run count per table, takes a full export automatically, then asks the super admin to type the venue name to confirm. Purge hard-deletes the selected rows in dependency order inside one transaction, resets affected sequences, and leaves the database consistent: ledger totals re-verified, no orphaned rows. The purge is written to the audit log, which purge never deletes. "Reset to seed" purges a venue and reloads the design-prompt seed.

**Backups and exports** (lane A): as in the tech plan, plus the nightly full export written to separate storage.

These override anything narrower in the earlier specification documents.
