**RIVR tech plan for the 15 Nov launch**

**Summary**

All 30 items in Rahul's note can be live for a 15 Nov opening, on three conditions. The build runs as four parallel lanes from 12 Oct, each against a fixed data contract. Three items are bought or assembled rather than built (7, 10 and 30), and three ship as a first version whose limits are stated below (12, 17 and 25). Approvals, accounts, hardware and content are assumed to arrive from Rahul when the lane needs them.[^1]

One date is earlier than the note asks for. The floor stack (tablet, cage, waitlist, TVs) goes live on 1 Nov in a practice venue so dealers and cashiers train on the system they will use on 15 Nov. 10 Nov is feature freeze; 11 to 14 Nov is reserved for load, outage and restore drills, with no new features.

**Five design changes that absorb most of the additions**

Most of the 24 additions are the same few mechanisms used in different places. Building the mechanisms once is what makes the date possible.

**Venue on every record.** Every table carries venue_id and row-level security filters by it. Item 29 (multi-room) costs a column now and a migration later, so it goes in on day one.

**Practice venue.** Training (item 18) is a second venue seeded with dummy members, tables and money. It runs the real code, is flagged non-financial, and is excluded from reports, Tally, GST, Meta events and investor statements. The certification quiz runs inside it.

**One event outbox.** Every business event (hand logged, high hand approved, tournament finished, visit closed, promotion published) writes one outbox row. Workers fan it out to WhatsApp, social publishing, winner cards, Meta Conversions API, feedback, Google review requests and Tally. Items 7, 8, 11, 15, 16 and 26 are consumers of this outbox, not separate systems.

**One graphics renderer.** The brand kit (items 5 and 6) is built as parameterised templates rendered server-side to PNG and MP4. The same templates produce TV slides, social posts, winner and high-hand cards, player share cards, the digital membership card and the receipt. Items 6, 7, 8, 9 and 28 share one renderer.

**Strings in catalogues from the first commit.** All copy lives in message catalogues keyed by locale. Hindi (item 19) is then a translation file, a per-user and per-dealer language setting, and a second set of WhatsApp templates, rather than a retrofit.

Public surfaces (website, live floor widget, link-in-bio, TV) read only from sanitised database views with no personal data, so item 14 and the website calendar need no separate API.

**Items shipping as a first version**

• Item 12: the platform provides the overlay and the delay; encoding and streaming run from OBS on the production machine. An in-app streaming pipeline is not worth building.

• Item 17: the recorder's playback link opens the clip at the right second. Pulling clips into the console follows once the recorder's API is confirmed on site.

• Item 25: a browser cannot keep reporting location once the screen locks, so the driver side is a small Android build with a foreground service. The player side stays in the PWA.

• Items 10 and 30 are bought and integrated, not built. Building an inbox or a store would take a lane-week each and add nothing a player sees.

• Item 7: platforms where the publishing API is not available to us at launch get a ready-to-post image and caption in the console, one tap to download.

**Two items changed in the build**

Item 15 is built unconditionally. If item 26 routed unhappy players to the General Manager and only happy players to Google, that is review gating, which Google's policy now lists as a violation with penalties up to profile restriction. The feedback ticket and the review request are independent outbox consumers.[^3]

Item 8 publishes nothing until the named player consents, per card, in the app. Consent is recorded with the card so a withdrawn consent can take the post down.

**Where the plan breaks, and the cut order**

• Review capacity is the real constraint. Four lanes produce more code than one person can review in depth, so money paths (lane A, cage and tablet in lane B) get line-by-line review and golden tests; lanes C and D get review by acceptance test.

• 2 to 10 Nov is the most loaded week, with 18 items landing across all four lanes. If it slips, cut in this order, all of which can switch on after opening without touching money: 13 social wall, 11 channel analytics, 12 stream overlay, 30 store, 25 cab app (manual drop calls in the meantime), 24 F&B ordering (paper KOT), 19 Hindi for the player app, Learn drills.

• Nothing on the money path moves: KYC, ledger, cage, wallet, markers, TDS switch, GST invoices, dealer tablet, waitlist, backups with a tested restore, practice venue.

• Hardening week is not slack. If features run into 11 Nov, the restore drill and the paper-outage drill still happen before launch, and the last feature waits.
