# 0443: The v3 rail, screens with tabs, and the old addresses

Date: 3 Nov 2026. Console v3 brief, phase 1: the route map, the rail order, roles, and a redirect for every old address.

- **Rail.** Tonight (Live floor, Waitlist, Door, Food and drink), Players (Members, KYC review, Host queue, Feedback), Marketing (Promotions, Rewards, Social, Content), then Admin folded (Staff, Tables, Settings; Kill switch, Practice reset and Purge in danger text, by `control.engage` and `purge.run`, so super admins only). Screens hidden by decision 0284 stay off it and are refused by address under their own names (#811).
- **A host has no Marketing.** Rewards opens for `rewards.manage` or `points.give` (its tab) and `promotions.manage` (Spin wheel), no longer for `rewards.hand_over` alone. The prototype and brief take Marketing away from the host; handing over a redemption on the console is now for the floor, cashiers and admins. A follow-up can give the host hand-over on the Door if the club wants it.
- **Tabs in the address.** A screen that holds others names its tab `?tab=` (`/door?tab=players`), as before (#605). A tab with parts of its own names them `?part=`: Settings' areas (`/settings?tab=settings&part=floor`) and Devices' kinds (`/settings?tab=devices&part=tablets`). Each tab has its own permissions; a tab outside the role is refused by address under the screen's name; a screen shows only its role's tabs.
- **Title, tabs, then the tab.** As in the prototype, a screen with tabs shows its own title, then its pill tabs, then the tab's screen: `Screen`'s `tabs` slot. A `Screen` inside it is a part, its title heard as an h2 but not shown again (the tab says it), its actions and states as before, so tab screens need no change. A role with only one of the tabs sees that tab's screen alone, under its own title. On the console below 768 px the header names the screen, so a screen's h1 is heard there but not shown.
- **Old addresses.** Each redirects to its screen and tab: `/check-in` and `/players-today` to the Door, `/spin-wheel` to Rewards, `/website` and `/learn-sources` to Content, `/devices`, `/tvs`, `/tablets` and `/games-history` to Settings, `/tournament-interest` to `/waitlist?tab=interest`, `/dealer-pins` to Staff. `/rake` opens Settings and `/cameras` and `/computers` Devices, each saying in a line that it is no longer on the console.
- **Requests.** `/top-ups` and `/time-requests` open `/requests`, the phone's Requests tab, which holds today's two screens by tab until the Live floor's requests list (helper 2) replaces them. The header's request count links there too.
