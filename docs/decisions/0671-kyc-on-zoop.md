# 0671 KYC runs on Zoop.one; no vendor liveness; DigiLocker waits for Zoop

Date: 16 Nov 2026. The owner, 15 Nov: "We are using Zoop.one for KYC verification." Asked how liveness should work: keep the in-app selfie, and do not use Zoop's hosted liveness page. Supersedes the vendor in decision 0197; its duplicate rule (name, date of birth and last four digits) stands. Task #1020.

- **Vendor.** `createZoopKyc` calls Zoop with the headers `app-id` and `api-key`, a fresh `task_id` and the member's consent in every body. A real venue gets it when three function secrets are set: `ZOOP_APP_ID`, `ZOOP_API_KEY` and `ZOOP_ENVIRONMENT` (`test`, the host RIVR has called, or `live`, as Zoop's documentation names it). Without them KYC stays closed (decision 0014). The practice venue keeps the fake. Cashfree's KYC code is gone; its payouts went on 15 Nov.
- **PAN.** Pan Lite (valid, and whose), then Pan Demographic (the date of birth), as Zoop's 2022 documentation shows them; they are not on the account yet. Zoop gives the holder's name, not a match, so `namesMatch` (`@rivr/domain/identity`) decides: the same words in any order, case and punctuation ignored. A missing or extra word, such as a middle name, is no match: the safest reading, asked in #1021. A name that differs settles the check, so its date of birth is not asked.
- **Face match.** The selfie against the Aadhaar photo, as Zoop's test host answered on 15 Nov: 100 with a score out of 100 is a match (Zoop's own threshold decides); 101 is not the same person, or no face in an image.
- **No liveness.** `checkLiveness` is optional on `KycAdapter`. Zoop's selfie is kept with no score and is not a paid call; the gate still runs (consent, the at-the-club switch), and since `20261122100000_kyc_record_on_an_unpaid_call.sql` an unpaid call makes the member's check as a paid one did. Nothing then stops a photo of a photo but the face match: a matching DigiLocker check verifies at once (decision 0400), with no staff look. It does not wait for staff: the owner, answering #1024 (decision 0677; decision 0675 had made it wait).
- **DigiLocker.** Not on RIVR's Zoop account and not documented in public, so it answered `not_configured` (closed) until it was built from Zoop's real replies. Built since #1023: decision 0676. `kyc.at_the_club` stays on until it is walked on staging and live keys are set.
- **Failures and spend.** 429 is rate limited; 5xx and Zoop's 99 and 108–111 (its 2022 list) are unavailable, and the member's paid call is given back; any other code is a refusal noted as `Zoop <code>` (`Zoop 403` is a product not on the account, `Zoop 112` a low wallet in the 2022 list). A billed answer RIVR cannot read (`Zoop unreadable`), or a date-of-birth call that fails after Pan Lite was paid for, is a refusal, so it is not given back.
- **Images.** Fetched only over https, as `data:` URLs, or over http on this machine; no redirect, 15 seconds, 5 MB at most.
- **Production's host.** Since #1022 every release sets `ZOOP_ENVIRONMENT=live` on rivr-prod and no longer sets `CASHFREE_ENVIRONMENT`, which nothing reads; the variable `PROD_CASHFREE_ENVIRONMENT` and decision 0421's sandbox exception are gone with it.
