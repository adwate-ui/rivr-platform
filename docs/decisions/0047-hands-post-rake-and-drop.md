# 0047 A hand posts its rake and drop out of chips in play; the jackpot is live while a drop is set

Date: 19 Oct 2026. Task #42.

A logged hand moves money. The rake and the jackpot drop are chips the dealer pulls from the pot, so they leave play: each posts as its own balanced ledger transaction, debiting `venue.chips_outstanding` (chips in play, a liability) and crediting `venue.rake` (revenue) or `venue.jackpot.main` (the bad-beat jackpot, players' money). Keys are the hand's (`hand:<id>:rake`, `hand:<id>:drop`), so a replayed hand posts nothing twice; nothing posts for a nought. The jackpot's sub-pools take their shares of the drop with #48; lane A owns the chart of accounts and may move these legs, but not their sum.

The rules come from the effective-dated settings at the hand's own time (`played_at`, which the tablet sends): `rake.config` for the table's game and `jackpot.drop` for the venue. The spec drops into the jackpot "once the bad-beat jackpot is live" and no setting says when that is, so the jackpot is live while the venue sets a drop above zero; setting it to zero stops the drop (question #295). A drop the pot cannot pay after the rake is refused with the hand (question #296). A hand also records whether the cap applied: the rate's share of the pot was more than the cap (a share exactly at the cap is not capped), as @rivr/domain's `potTake` and its SQL twin `private.pot_take` both say, proved on `packages/testing/golden/rake.json`.

A hand belongs to one down: the open down begun on the tablet that logs it, whose dealer may still log hands, and `played_at` falls within it (a minute either side for the tablet's clock). So a tablet cannot backdate a hand to cheaper rules or file it under another dealer. A down ends when its tablet is moved, freed or revoked, and when its dealer's access is revoked or they are made inactive. A pot is whole ₹50 chips, up to ₹1 crore; nothing is logged while the venue is frozen, though a replay still answers with the hand already logged. Hands are append-only, and only staff who read the ledger see them; the tablet reads its own down's hands through `down_hands`. Hands that reach the server after their down has ended are B-013's (the offline queue).
