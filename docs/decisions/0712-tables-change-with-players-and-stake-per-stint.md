# 0712 Tables change with players seated, and each stretch keeps its stake

Date: 25 Nov 2026. The owner's request of 25 Nov and their answers (#1137, #1138).

- **The floor sets up tables.** The floor role holds `tables.manage`: everything in Tables setup, as admins. A stake's minimum playing time is set with the stake there, so `private.set_stake_call_time` takes `tables.manage` as well as `settings.manage`.
- **Changed with players seated.** A table's stake changes with players seated or waiting; seats, name and minimum buy-in already did. Seated players stay where they are, with their minimum playing time and call time; the waiting list stays with the table, its places moving to the new stake as a list's places are at its table's. Fewer seats than one taken stays refused (the table's trigger), and the console names the seat from the floor's seats.
- **Each stretch keeps its stake.** `seat_session.table_type_id` is the current stretch's stake and `seat_move.from_table_type_id` the stake of a stretch a move ended. A stake change writes a seat_move on the same table and seat for each seat taken, so the seat goes on as one session. `private.session_tables` gives each stretch's stake, read by play-time Bonus, rake shares, a member's sessions and the promotion replay.
- **The past repaired.** The owner ruled that relabeling past nights is unacceptable. Every existing stretch got the stake its table had when it began, from game_table's audit log (`private.table_stake_at`), with the tables' triggers held during the repair. Production before release: 2 tables changed stake once each, 33 seats on them, 5 of which showed the new stake for play at the old; no stake in their history was deleted. Bonus already decided stays.
- **A stake games were played at stays** (RVHST), as their history keeps it.
- **Around a change.** A member's sessions show a row for each stake played at a table that visit (the first keeps the table's session id, so a hidden session stays hidden). A leave undone after the change goes on at the new stake; a seat taken just before one is not undone. A hand sent late is raked by the stake its table had when played. A table's past stake is read from game_table's audit log in the log's own order. A member under a freeze has their seat and place moved to the new stake with everyone's: the change is the table's, as ending a seat is the floor's.
- Supersedes 0467's line that a table's stake does not change while its list has places.
- **Read at the stretch's stake as well:** the seat Bonus sweep (the seat's first stretch, though it runs minutes later), the high-hand replay (the table's stake at the hit), and a seat ended by a correction before a stake change (its stretches end there). In the Players report and on the Door a stake change goes on in the seat's row; a change of seat is a row of its own, as before.
- Not changed: editing a stake's blinds still edits them for its past nights (question #1139).
