# 0453: Website pages by group, resolved feedback, and a call's reason

Date: 3 Nov 2026. Console v3, phases 4 and 5 (Content, Feedback, Host queue, Rewards).

- **The duplicate "RIVR Poker Club"** in the website's page list was two pages with that title: Home and the link-in-bio page. The list now names them Home and Link in bio.
- **Page groups** come from the page's address, as pages have no kind: Legal is privacy, terms and responsible play; Guides is the journal, its articles and questions; the rest is Club. A search narrows the list.
- **Feedback's Resolved tab** holds tickets resolved, with who resolved them and what was done, and, for the last 90 days, ratings that needed no ticket. Its count covers both.
- **A host call needs a reason** (why they stopped coming) before Save the call; the console refuses without one and says so. The database still accepts a call without one, so the rule is the screen's: a follow-up can make it the database's.
- **The spin wheel** says why a prize is off it ("Off the wheel: this month's spin budget cannot pay it") with a link to the spin budget setting. That setting is hidden on Settings today (stored in paise, decision 0284); Settings must show it as bonus chips a month for the link to land on it.
