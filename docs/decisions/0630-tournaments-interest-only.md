# 0630. Tournaments are interest only

Redesign RD-26 (#969), owner's brief of 15 Nov: "create (name, start, game), see who asked to be notified, send the notification, keep a history with entries. Nothing else." Decided while building:

- **The old module's database side is gone**: templates, blind structures, entries, purchases, payouts and tickets, the clock's functions, the ticket-lapse job, `@rivr/domain/tournament` with its golden files, and the cash limits (`enforce_cash_limits`, `aml_cash_in`, `cash_limit_hit` and `cashLimitHit`), which a tournament purchase was the last to reach. The migration refuses to run where the club holds a tournament purchase or payout made since its fresh start of 12 Nov; older ones are staging's test rows (one purchase of 28 Oct) and go with the tables, as decision 0587 took a high hand and a marker. Production holds none of any date. Left, as something kept still reads them: the ledger's tournament accounts and `day_tournament_fees` in the night's invoice (always nothing now), the setting `cage.cash_buy_in_limit`, and the website calendar's two money columns, always empty.
- **A tournament is a name, a start and a game.** Its stake and description are dropped with the buy-in. The game is picked from No-Limit Hold'em and Pot-Limit Omaha and kept as text, as before.
- **Notify sends once**, a `member.notice` named `tournament_starts` to each member who asked: a push and a WhatsApp message with fixed words, "RIVR Sunday starts Sun 15 Nov, 16:00." The prototype's typed message is not built: a member never gets free text from the club's number (decision 0303). With nobody to tell, nothing is sent and the one send is kept.
- **Who hears of a tournament is one rule** (`private.hears_of_tournaments`): not erased, not on a break, tournaments in their tier, and their own Tournaments switch on. It now governs a withdrawal and a move too, which WhatsApp sent whatever the switch said. The count in the list is the members told.
- **Nobody is registered.** "Mark registered", its Undo, Edit and "Publish again" go. Cancel tournament stays (the prototype has it): it tells those who asked and takes the tournament off both lists. A wrong start is cancelled and created again.
- **Entries are a number staff type** on a tournament that has started: no entry is recorded as it is made, so nothing else can know it. The prototype's result list of places is not built.
- **A member's Sessions show the past tournaments they asked to be notified of**, with the entries typed, and no place, time or money: who played is recorded nowhere, so the app never says a member played. A month's sessions and hours count cash sessions alone. Question for the owner: these, every past tournament, or none.
- **`tournaments.manage` stays with super admins and admins.** The prototype lists Tournaments for the marketing role: a question for the owner.
- **The Waitlist keeps one tab**, so its count is the waitlist's alone; `/tournament-interest` opens Tournaments.
