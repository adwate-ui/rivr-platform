# 0693 Bonus reversed, and a use added after the fact

Date: 19 Nov 2026. Issue #1077. The owner, 18 Nov: on the Members page, reverse a Bonus given and add a Bonus use after the fact. Super admins only. A reversal takes back the whole amount even if part was spent, and the balance may go below zero until new Bonus covers it. The owner's rulings of 19 Nov settle the rest.

- **Reversal** (`reverse_bonus`): the grant's whole amount, less any part already expired (that went back then), kept as `bonus_grant.reversal_paise`. It is taken from what is left of that grant first, ended or not, then from the member's other Bonus, soonest to end first (`bonusTake`, SQL twin `private.bonus_takes`). Whatever they cannot cover is owed. One ledger transaction takes it back, once by its idempotency key. The ledger's `reverses_transaction_id` is kept for an exact opposite, so the transaction is not linked through it. A reversal is refused (RVNIL) when nothing is left to take back.
- **Missed use** (`add_bonus_use`): any seat of the member's, from any visit, once past its Undo window; whole fifties and a reason. There is no visit cap, but the use counts toward that visit's cap for later uses. Reports count it on the seat's start (`bonus_redemption.used_at`). The console reads the seats ten at a time, up to the newest 500, as the Bonus history read is capped.
- **Owed**: `bonus_owed` keeps add-only signed rows. A reversal or a use adds what it could not take. Bonus given since pays it off first (`paid`, `bonusPayOff`, `private.bonus_pay_off`). No Bonus is used at a buy-in meanwhile (RVBON).
- **Two balances**: `private.bonus_balance` is what members and staff see: Bonus in force less what is owed, below zero while more is owed. `private.bonus_in_force` is unchanged and serves draws, the visit cap and expiry.
- **Reports** count a reversal on the day it was made: past days never change. That day's Bonus given, its chart, the Bonus report and the door's and visits' Bonus fall by it, and can be below zero. The Bonus given list shows it as its own row, below zero, "Reversed by" the super admin.
- **Promotions**: a reversed promotion's grant becomes `reversed`. Its slot is free, so the member can earn it again, and the promotion's limits, budget, MIS and overview no longer count it. The giver's daily by-hand allowance is not given back.
- **The member** sees "Reversed" and a missed use's "Buy-in · Table", with no reason. A reversal raises no toast; a missed use raises the usual buy-in toast if it is their newest line. The reasons stay on the record and in Activity, and members read neither: column grants keep `bonus_grant.reversal_reason` and `bonus_redemption.reason` from them (and from Realtime), the ledger memo is fixed ("Bonus reversed", "Bonus use added"), and their export gives the reasons as staff notes (decision 0240).
- **On erasure**: Bonus a reversal or a use left owed does not hold an erasure back. It is written off as the member is erased: one ledger transaction (`member_erasure_forfeit:owed:<member>`) and a `written_off` row. A ledger balance owed beyond that, which no Bonus record explains, still holds it back, as before. The reversal's reason is scrubbed with the rest of the person. The erasure preview names what will be forfeited after that write-off. Erasure takes the member's Bonus lock before the member's row, as every Bonus change does.
- Both actions are business events (`bonus.reversed`, `bonus.use_added`). No consumer claims them yet, so nothing is pushed. Both obey the promotions kill switch, refuse a staff member's own record and are rate-limited (RVRAT).
