# 0565. Settings say their defaults, and what the club does not run is not offered

Date: 9 Nov 2026. From the 180-day audit: F-031, F-070, F-084, F-086, F-113, F-120, F-127, F-214.

**A rule on its default says so**: `30 minutes (default)`. The default lives on the setting's contract. `Not set` stays for a rule that is off until set.

**Feature overrides offer only what the club runs**, by one list in contracts, as the TV picker has `HIDDEN_TV_MODES`.

**The Academy graduate tier is retired and its two drafts deleted.** Its members move to Member, each with a line in their history. A new venue still gets the tier from its bootstrap; RIVR has one venue, so that was left. A tier change that ends and would return someone to a retired tier sends them to the venue's starting tier.

**Erasure no longer waits on an Academy booking**, which no screen can clear. The money blockers stay: while the cage is hidden they cannot fire, and if money ever exists a member must not be erased with it outstanding.

**F-214 is not done, on the owner's word.** The audit asked to stop linking WhatsApp leads to members. The owner's answer on #585 (28 Oct) is that WhatsApp leads link by phone, and decision 0268 has marketing read the member each lead became.
