# 0607 Four more forms in the panel at the side

Date: 15 Nov 2026. Issue 982 (redesign RD-38; correction 1 of `docs/spec/redesign-2026-11-15.md`; the owner, 15 Nov: "yes, all four").

- **Staff.** Add staff opens the form in the panel; added, it says so in a toast and closes, where it used to stay open under a line of text. A name in the table opens that person in the panel, where they used to open beside the table, with what the manager may change shown at once: the row's own Change and Done are gone, since the panel opens and closes it.
- **Notices.** "New notice" opens the form in the panel, with the preview under the fields; Edit on a row opens the same panel on that notice. Put up or saved, the panel closes and the screen says so. Ending a notice stays on its row. A notice can no longer be ended while it is being edited, since the panel covers the list.
- **Feedback.** Resolve, for the tickets ticked, opens the panel with "What was done".
- **The menu.** Add to the menu opens the panel. The menu has no form that changes an item's name or kind, so there is nothing more to move.
- Each panel's foot has Cancel and the form's one gold button, as Tables setup and Settings have. The fields, their words, their checks and who may use them are unchanged.
- The issue's third line asks that no form in the console creates or changes a record inside the page, the promotion builder aside. The four screens it names are done; a search finds others it does not name (pairing a TV, a tablet and a camera, Club details, a dealer's PIN, a player added to a waitlist, the tournament forms, a KYC decision, the website's editor, and the forms docked in the member record). Those are listed in a follow-up issue and not changed here.
