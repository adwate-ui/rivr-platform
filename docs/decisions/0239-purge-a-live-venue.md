# 0239: Purging a live venue deletes its play and its people's data, after a fresh archive, while it is frozen

Date: 2026-10-27 · Task: #432 (features "Purge") · The owner approved the plan, and chose on 27 Oct that a purge removes everything personal it leaves behind.

**Who and when.** A super admin with `purge.run`, at a venue that is not the practice one (that one resets to its seed, #104), while the venue is frozen.

**The archive first, and fresh.** The console makes the full encrypted archive (#103), reading every table to its last page (the database notes each), saves the file and says so with its SHA-256 and size (`complete_export_archive`: its maker, still allowed to export, every table read). The purge needs such an archive of that venue, by the same super admin, started after the freeze, saved in the last 30 minutes, and every table it deletes from still holding the rows the archive counted (`RVARC` otherwise): a freeze stops money and play, not sign-ups or feedback. That archive is the only copy of what the purge deletes, the AML register (decision 0234's "never deleted") and KYC records included, so a purge belongs before launch, and the archive is kept as long as the law asks.

**What goes.** What the spec's purge names: members, visits, sessions, hands, tournaments, the ledger's entries, promotions, media and messages: every table a practice reset reloads, but for what a live venue keeps as setup. With the members go their freezes (the kill switch's link to a member now cascades), their logins (unless one is also staff or a device, as member erasure decides), the personal fields of the audit rows about what was deleted (stripped as erasure strips them; the rows saying what happened stay), and, through the `purge-venue` Edge Function, the venue's files in Storage: KYC photos and expense receipts. If the files do not all go, the screen offers to remove them again.

**What stays.** What a reset keeps (setup, staff, controls, cameras, the audit log), and the venue's own ledger accounts (its members' accounts go), payment accounts, shareholders and holdings, Tally map, blind structures, tournament templates, expense categories, reward catalogue and League seasons. `150_purge_live_venue` lists the tables, so a new one is a decision.

**How.** The dry run counts each table's rows; the venue's name is typed (`RVNAM`). One statement deletes it all, foreign keys checked at its end, past the append-only guards for that venue and transaction only (`rivr.purge`, decision 0117). Nothing may be left, nor a ledger entry of the venue. The audit log records the counts and the owner is alerted (`venue.purged`). Shared id sequences are left alone (0117); per-venue numbering starts again, since its rows go. Date-range and per-entity scopes from the spec are not in #432's acceptance.
