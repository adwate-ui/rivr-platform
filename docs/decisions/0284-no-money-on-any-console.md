# 0284: No rupee or rake on any console

Date: 29 Oct 2026. Owner's feedback doc of 29 Oct ("RIVR App | Detailed Feedback"), phase E of the Fix Tracker, issues #626 to #647. Its core rule: "No mention of rupee, rake anywhere on any console. No financial settlement, MIS, reporting, tracking required anywhere."

- **Hide; don't delete.** The ledger, cage, rake, tax and tournament code and their database tests stay. The screens go. A hidden console screen is marked `hidden` in the rail: it is off the rail, and its address is refused to everyone. Its code stays for when the club wants it back.
- **Hidden in the console** (the owner's list, plus what still showed money):
  - the owner dashboard, Tournaments, Dealers, Jackpots and High hands;
  - all of Cage, Money and Reports;
  - Players' Tiers;
  - Marketing's League, Events, Creators and Academy;
  - Team's Roster, Certification, SOPs and Incidents;
  - Exports;
  - Settings' rake, cage, tax, compliance, academy, system and chips areas, and the rules that turn rake or money into points or bonus;
  - Devices' computers and cameras;
  - the live floor's rake and rupee figures, F&B's Glaza statement and Social's winner cards.
- **Hidden in the player app** (#627): the Balance tab with deposits and withdrawals, the Academy's batches and payments, winner cards, Home's jackpot, and every buy-in, prize, pool and ticket value. Sessions show when, the tables and how long, and a member can hide one from their own list; the club keeps it. Bonus amounts read as chips (`formatChips`). The hidden addresses go to Tonight or Learn.
- **Hidden on the TV** (#628): the jackpot mode (`HIDDEN_TV_MODES`: the console does not offer it, and a TV still set to it shows the waitlist board), the tournament clock's prize pool and the spin wheel's rupee prizes, which read as bonus chips.
- **Points by hand only** (#629): no points for hours played or a share of rake, and no weekly rakeback. A migration sets every points rate in force to zero, the seed gives none, and the rakeback job is unscheduled; the rules' code stays. The website leaves out a zero points rate and still shows rakeback, at the owner's word. Every bonus a console shows reads as bonus chips (`formatChips`).
- **The tablet is optional** (#630): it keeps the seats, call time, away and back, leaving and food, all of which the console does too (it gained Away and Back). Hand entry, buy-in requests and the drop-box count are off it; a down ends with nothing counted.
- **Also off the console:** the high-hand claim prompt across the top of every screen (it asked to approve a payout in rupees). Devices keeps its Tablets tab, as the tablet stays (#630).
- **Promotion words:** the promotions a venue starts with say bonus chips, not rupees, and RIVR Rewards no longer promises points from rake.
- **Left to later batches:** top-up times per table come with #639, and Learn's own points (`learn.session_points`) stop when #637 switches Learn off.
- **Learn, the Academy and Events off** (#637): by their feature keys, `learn.library`, `academy.booking` and `events.book`, for every tier from now, hidden rather than shown locked, and off for a new venue's tiers. They come back as any tier feature does. The rest of Learn's features keep their cells. The website's Academy page still links to the app, where the address goes to Learn: the owner decides it with the website.
- **Tournaments by interest** (#636): Home lists the club's upcoming published tournaments, with no buy-in, and a member registers interest; they pay at the venue, and the floor marks them registered on the console's Tournament interest screen (waitlist.manage). The tournament card, its buy-ins from the balance and the satellite tickets are off Home.
- **At the table** (#634): call time is three hours from now for every game that had less (a setting the club still changes). A seated member adds an hour of call time while it runs (never once it is done, so a member who met the rule never comes to owe time) and leaves from the app (`table.leave`, a core feature): with call time owed they go on the Leaving list, and the seat opens when the floor lets them go. A top-up has no amount: the member asks (`table.top_up`), the floor marks it in progress on the Live floor and resets it once done.
- **Joining is a request** (#633): a join from the app waits for the floor, which approves it onto the list from the console's Waitlist, in the order the member asked, or removes it; a member the floor adds is on the list at once. A request has no place, is not counted on Home and is called by no one. A member waits for one game at a time (`RVONE`), as they sit at one table at a time: seated, they join no list from the app, though the floor may add them to another game's, to change tables (#77); question to the owner in the PR. A request is called by no one. Tonight's tables show each table's stakes, open seats, call time and wait.
- **Sign-up asks more** (#631): the number signed in with, an optional photo, the name, the city, what best describes the member (one) and when they usually play (any), kept in `member_preference`. The photo goes beside the member's identity images in the private kyc bucket (`<venue>/<member>/photo.jpg`), written once by the kyc Edge Function as the selfie is (a JPEG without its metadata, after `begin_sign_up_photo` checks the member, the freeze and the sign-up switch) and read by no client, so erasure and the purge remove it with them and no new bucket needs their code.
- **The floor's night** (E3, #638 to #641):
  - **Table runs.** Each table's opening and closing is kept as a run (`table_run`), for games history (#643).
  - **Call time.** The floor sets a seated player's call time on the Live floor, in minutes. Adding to it is the floor's; cutting it needs the admin's override, as letting a player leave early does. A call time already played out is not changed, so a player who met it never comes to owe time. A practice reset keeps the tables' runs with the tables.
  - **Join requests.** The Live floor counts the joins waiting and leads to the Waitlist, where the floor approves them.
  - **Top-ups.** Each request is kept (`top_up_request`). The Live floor lists tonight's by table for the floor and the cashier: requested, in progress, fulfilled with the time it took, reset, or left open by a player who left.
  - **Service.** A new role, Service, holds `fnb.serve`: the menu, tonight's orders, accepting and delivering them, and handing a KOT to the bar. It never comps an order, nor reads Glaza's statement, which stay with `fnb.manage`. It lands on Food and drink, and the Live floor's address is refused to it. It serves orders in three lists (to accept, being prepared, delivered), each with its table, seat and time. Staff with `fnb.manage`, the admin and the super admin, read the same; the Floor role of #644 gets it there. Its stewards acknowledge a Food and drink service SOP, as every role has one.
  - **Today's players.** A screen of every member at the club this club day: status, check-in and check-out, the time they waited, and each table with the time there.
- **Games you play** (#653, the owner, 1 Nov): sign-up asks which the member plays, any of poker, PLO, bomb pots and ROE, kept with the other answers.
- **The super admin's tools** (E4, #642 to #647):
  - **Tables setup.** A permission of its own, `tables.manage` (super admin and admin), as the floor runs the tables but does not set them up. A stake is a table type: its game and blinds in whole 50s of chips. Its code comes from them (`plo_200_400`), its buy-in range from the big blind (buy-ins are off the consoles, so nobody is asked), and it starts with three hours of call time. A table has a name of its own, a stake and 2 to 10 seats, and starts last in floor order and closed. Its stake changes only while nobody sits or is called there. Tables and table types are written only through these functions: the API's direct writes are gone (the floor opens and closes tables through `set_table_open`).
  - **Games history.** From the table runs (#638): each table on each club day it opened, the times it opened and how long it ran, the last seven club days at first.
  - **Points by hand.** A permission of its own, `points.give` (super admin, admin, Floor and Cashier; not marketing, who keeps the catalogue). Staff with it give a member points as earned or as locked, and release locked ones into earned, each once per request id, never to themselves; each change is an outbox event. Locked points sit in an account of their own (`member.points_locked`), so a redemption, which spends `member.points`, never spends them, and erasure forfeits them with the rest. The member's Rewards shows what stays locked. The copy says release: unlock is a banned word.
  - **The hours bonus.** The builder's fourth reward: after the hours played in a visit (1 to 12), the bonus chips (whole 50s), for a week. It is the engine's `hours_played` trigger with `min_hours`, as the club's hours bonus template.
  - **Roles.** A new role, Floor: the Live floor, the Waitlist and Check-in, call time, Today's players, food and drink orders, and Rewards with points by hand. The cashier gains the same less the orders. Neither sees tables setup, the member list, games history, tournaments or bonus: the member list moves to `members.manage` (the host keeps it, by `host_queue.manage`), and Tournament interest to `tournaments.manage` and the spin wheel to `promotions.manage`, in the database too. The doc's Devices module (TVs only) waits for a TV-only permission: `devices.manage` also enrols the cage's and admin's computers and keeps the cameras, so neither role gets it. The admin role stays as it was; the owner moves floor managers to Floor on the Staff screen.
  - **The CRM is HubSpot, not the console.** The `crm-sync` outbox consumer upserts each member as a contact, keyed by `rivr_member_id`, after they sign up, save their sign-up answers, finish the identity check or end a visit. Each run first queues any member not yet sent (`member.crm_sync`, once each), so every member arrives, not only those with an event in the claim's 30 days. It is the club's own member record, so it does not wait for marketing consent (a question to the owner). Saved answers tell it once an hour a member at most. An erasure at a live venue emits `member.erased` (the member's id only), and the consumer deletes the contact for good through HubSpot's GDPR delete: erasure now reaches the vendor, where before it sent nothing out. Until `HUBSPOT_ACCESS_TOKEN` is set it answers 503 and claims nothing. The owner makes a private app with the contacts write scope and the `rivr_` contact properties `toHubSpotProperties` names.
- **Staff stays** for staff managers. Staff accounts, their roles and the dealers' PINs have no other home.
- **The website keeps** its rupee buy-ins, jackpot and rakeback, at the owner's word. The tablet stays, but every workflow runs without it.
- **e2e:** flows whose only purpose was a hidden screen are removed; their database sides stay in pgTAP. Flows that only passed through one, such as call time through the cage, now run the console's own path. With the practice cage's flows gone, CI's fourth e2e runner for `@practice-cage` (decision 0243) is gone too: the three shards run every flow.
