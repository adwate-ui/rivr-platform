# 0602. Settings in groups, and typed values

Redesign RD-18 and RD-19 (#961, #962), owner 15 Nov. Decided while building, and after the review of the pull request.

- **A setting is shown where it has a group** (`groups.ts`): Floor has Seating, Time at the table and Identity; Members has Bonus and Membership; TVs has Rotation, under the tab's title and above its screens, for staff who change settings. Door code refresh and dealer rotation have none: they go with the card and with dealers. The prototype's "ID check before the first seat", "Boards shown" and "Time on each board" are not settings today and are not built here.
- **A row opens the setting's panel on the right** (correction 1). Change, for staff who may change it: the new value first, then what is in force, to come and before. History, for a setting a staff member only reads (a super admin's, seen by an admin): the same panel with no form and nothing to withdraw, so nobody loses the history they could read before (#997).
- **Cancel and Save are in the panel's foot.** The panel stays open after a save and says "Change saved.", so a change to come can be withdrawn from the same panel. Save sends once however often it is pressed. What the database refuses is said beside what it concerns: the value under its fields, the moment under When, anything else under the form; it clears when anything is edited.
- **A change starts now or at a typed day and time**, in India's time, as Notices type them; a moment that has passed is refused before it is sent, and by the database again.
- **One line a setting**, eight words or fewer, held as the setting's `help`; a test holds the count. It is under the name in the row and under the title in the panel. What a value means where its limits do not say ("0 is never.") is a hint under its field (`hints`). Groups have no line (correction 23).
- **The Google review link is removed**: the setting, and the message after a third visit. The table `review_request` and the `review.requested` event stay for #954, so this change does not touch member erasure.
- **Add time choices are whole minutes, 1 to 1,440, up to twelve**, and the list says so once it is full. A value set before today holds its minutes as text; a setting's rows are never rewritten, so it is read as numbers, by the database as before and by the console. With none set the list starts from 30 min, 1 h and 2 h, what the club runs on. A length typed twice is offered once. A length of 1,440 can be set and is always refused when asked for, since a seat with call time has a minute at least: the rule that a call time is a day at most is kept whole rather than bent to the list.
- **Numbers in a setting are read as numbers before they are whole.** A value written with a point (30.0) passes its schema, so the lengths, a stake's call time and both Bonus limits are read through `numeric`.
- **Lengths are written in the short form** `@rivr/i18n` has ("1 h", "1 h 30 min"), on the player's Add time sheet too (correction 17), since any length may now be offered.
- **Call time is typed for every stake at once, in minutes**, the unit the stake's own form uses (decision 0601). Save sends only the stakes that changed; a stake left blank keeps its call time. Several stakes are several saves: when a later one is refused, Save again sends only those not yet among the changes to come for that moment. A stake since deleted keeps its rows and is shown nowhere: not in the row, the form or the stakes whose history the panel offers.
- **Seating starts from the stake's own call time on both seat sheets**, typed in minutes, 0 for none, which the field says; the three chips and their fallback to three hours are gone.
- **Bonus added by hand has its own cap**, `bonus.by_hand_cap`, on one addition. It binds admins and floor managers and not super admins (the owner, 15 Nov, #996), as the daily limit does not. Unset, the most any addition may be applies, 1,00,000, and the row says so. The form's hint says the most the person adding may add. The cap is a limit on the amount, so it answers before the daily limit: an addition above both is refused for the cap and alerts no one, and the next one under the cap meets the daily limit as before. The daily limit on one staff member stays as it is (correction 21).
