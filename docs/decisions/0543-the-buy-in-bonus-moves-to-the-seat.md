# 0543: The buy-in bonus moves to the seat

Date: 8 Nov 2026 · Audit of 8 Nov (F-005, F-033) · The owner, 8 Nov · Builds on decisions 0284, 0480, 0501 and 0509

**Why.** Nothing records a buy-in since the Cage was hidden, so a promotion paying on one never paid.

**What.** A seat bonus is a fixed number of bonus chips, never a percentage: on the first seat of a visit, or on the first N seats at a table in a club day. A sweep each minute pays a seat once it is past its two minutes of Undo, for seats of the last day, each weighed once, and a seat waits while the venue or member is frozen. It is paid at the table first sat at, in the club day it was taken, only by terms approved before it was taken. A seat released inside its two minutes pays nobody, nor does one a staff member gave their own member record.

**Safest reading of "the first N seats".** One place a member a club day (as decision 0501 read first N grants), so one member cannot take a table's places by leaving and sitting again. Question issue open for the owner.

**Different from the owner's words.** Fixed buy-in promotions were to become new versions awaiting approval. None exists (read 8 Nov: production drafts only, staging one live percentage promotion), and a version awaiting approval must have an author. So anything running that pays on a buy-in is paused, and drafts become seat bonus drafts to read again and submit. The migration logs how many.

**Also ended.** Drafts for a cab home, a tournament entry or an Academy graduate, none of which the club runs. The builder no longer offers them; a new venue's templates still make such drafts.
