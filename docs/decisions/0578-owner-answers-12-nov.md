# 0578: The owner's answers of 12 Nov 2026

The owner asked for five changes (#905 to #909) and answered how each works.

- **House rules (#905):** the section that starts "Poker is a game of skill" leaves the player app. The website's About page keeps it.
- **Website editor (#906):** a section can be added (any kind the editor can fill, at any place), deleted, moved and changed into another kind. The items of any list can be added and removed. Kinds that need a picture, a map link, a table's grid or a link into the app cannot be added, as the editor changes words only.
- **TV leaderboard (#907):** the period is a console setting (Tonight, This week, This month, All time). A member with "Name on TVs" off shows as "Member #17".
- **Bonus chips (#908):** each grant ends N days after it was given, the oldest spent first. At switch-on, chips already held end N days from then. At most x% of the balance held at the visit's first seat is redeemed in that visit.
- **Seat bonus tiers (#909):** counted over the promotion's life. The first X members to claim it get A chips, the next Y get B, and so on.

Also on 12 Nov: nothing goes to production until all five are in.
