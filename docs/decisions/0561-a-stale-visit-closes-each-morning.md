# 0561: A stale visit closes each morning

Date: 9 Nov 2026 · Audit of 8 Nov (F-016) · Builds on decision 0161

**Why.** A visit left open by a freeze closed only at that member's next check-in, so two stayed open for weeks.

**What.** A daily job at 11:10 IST closes any visit from an earlier club day with no seat open, at its last moment. Not at 06:00, because closing a visit can send the review request. A stop in force holds it, and the first run after release closes it.

**Not done, against the audit.** The audit also said to let check-out run during a freeze because it moves no money. It does move something: closing a visit credits its points and hotel nights, so decision 0161 stands and check-out stays refused. The job makes that harmless.
