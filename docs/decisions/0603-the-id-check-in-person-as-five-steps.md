# 0603. The ID check in person as five steps

Redesign RD-21 (#964), owner 15 Nov. Decided while building, and after the review of the pull request.

- **The bar starts once a member is chosen.** The search and "Due tonight" are how the desk finds the member; the five steps (Member, Face, PAN card, Aadhaar front, Details) are that member's check, under `Stepper look="progress"`.
- **Member is a step of its own**: the member's name and the end of their mobile number, beside their app selfie if they took one, then Start the check, or Choose another member, which drops what was taken and typed for this one.
- **"Use photo" moves on to the first photo still missing, else to Details.** A first pass goes Face, PAN card, Aadhaar front, Details; a photo taken again from Details comes back to Details, by Use photo or by Back. A photo made ready after the person has gone to another step is kept and moves nothing.
- **Back goes one step back and keeps everything.** A photo step whose photo is kept shows the photo, with Next and "Take the photo again", not its camera; a photo step has no Next until its photo is kept, so Details is reached only with all three.
- **Each step takes the focus as it opens**, on its name, heard and not seen: the button that led there is gone, and the focus would fall to the page behind the panel.
- **What is wrong is said where it is.** A photo not received, under "Photo 1 of 3", beside its camera. On Details, under each field and under the consent; editing anything clears the line a refusal from the server left under the step.
- **Verify sends once.** While the check is saved, Back and the three ways to take a photo again are off, so its answer comes back to the member it was sent for.
- **A check where only photos were sent back** (decision 0573) opens on the first of them in the steps' own order (the database lists them by name, the Aadhaar front first): the bar on that photo's step, its camera ready, and why it came back above it. Each photo is saved as it is kept, as before, the bar staying while it is; then the next one owed. Leaving the camera, or a photo that was not saved, shows what is still owed.
- **The checks, the words and the rules are the ones before**: the cameras (auto-capture, zoom, the Aadhaar's bars to confirm, the hand-off to a phone where there is no back camera), consent asked at the desk, and the gate in the database. New words are the five step names, "Start the check", "Choose another member", "Next" and the four lines under a field or the consent; "Take their photo" goes, since Start opens the camera.
- **Where the build differs from the renders on purpose**, because the issue keeps today's copy:
  - `desk-id-check-c01`: the name and mobile sit beside the selfie, as drawn; the caption "Selfie from the app" and the line "Ask for the original PAN card and Aadhaar..." are not added (the panel's subtitle already says "Originals in hand").
  - `c05`: no intro line ("Check each photo, then type the details...") and no captions under the two faces; the photos have their names for a screen reader, as before.
  - `c06`: the outcome stays the one line that is read out as it appears ("... is verified and can take a seat."). It is not a banner (`Banner` has no tone for good news, and an alert's would be wrong), there is no toast (it would say the same line twice), and the list under it stays as today: "Due tonight" on the full screen for the floor and hosts, none in the sheet, where the reviewer's queue is beside it. "Waiting for an ID check" would be a new heading.
