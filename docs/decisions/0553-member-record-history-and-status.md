# 0553: The member record's history and an account's status

Date: 9 Nov 2026 · From the 180-day audit of 8 Nov (I-71, I-72, I-74).

**Visits and points are lines, not tables.** The record docks in a 440 px panel, too narrow for five columns. Each visit is its day and tables with the time played, and a second line for the call time and whether they left early. Both lists come twenty at a time with Show earlier, as the bonus chips do.

**A departure withdrawn as a mistake (0500) does not count as leaving early.** A visit where no seat owed call time says nothing about it.

**The record's parts keep their names** (the owner, 9 Nov): bonus chips and points are distinct and the tab holds both. With all five showing, the strip scrolls sideways in the 440 px panel, as it did.

**An account's status is its own read,** beside the list's rows as Tonight is, rather than more columns on `members_of_tier`. Only members who are not simply active come back. Erased comes before frozen and frozen before a break, as each is what stops the member first. A member freeze shows only to staff who engage controls, as everywhere it shows (#493 P5), so anyone else reads a frozen member as they otherwise stand.
