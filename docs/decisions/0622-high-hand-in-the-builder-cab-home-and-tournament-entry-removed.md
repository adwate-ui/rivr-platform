# 0622. High hand in the promotion builder; cab home and tournament entry rewards removed

Issue #966 (RD-23), with the owner's ruling of 15 Nov on the reward types. Decided while building:

- **Removed whole, as the buy-in bonus was (0620).** The migration deletes every promotion made from the `cab` template or holding a cab home or tournament entry version, and says how many by status. Production holds one (practice venue, ended) and staging two (ended); none ever gave anything. It stops for a person to decide where one gave a grant, where a grant holds either perk, or where one also has a version of another kind or a rule. A grant's perk is waitlist priority alone, and a new venue gets no `cab` template.
- **Not part of this:** cab requests themselves (the feature `cab.requests`, the setting `cab.minimum_visits`, the Cab-home flow, the website's card). The old rule engine still names `tournament_entry` and `graduation` among its triggers; no rule uses them, and removing them is a follow-up.
- **The builder.** High hand is the fifth reward type, with a template: royal flush 25,000, straight flush 10,000, four of a kind 5,000, both rules held, four or more dealt in, 2,00,000 a month. Hands are added from the fixed list, kept in its order, and removed while more than one is left. Its Limits section is the budget alone, which it must have, whole or each month.
- **Words.** The card, on the list, Home and the TVs, says the most a hand pays: 'Up to 25,000 Bonus a hand'. The builder's one line names each hand: 'Royal flush 25,000, Four of a kind 5,000 Bonus, paid each time'. The list counts hands paid.
- **Its record opens beside the list** once it has a version running or run; a draft opens the builder as any promotion does. Paid tonight holds the night's hands and Bonus, the week's Bonus, what is left of the budget, tonight's hits and Log a high hand; Hands and rules holds each hand of the list with its Bonus or 'Not paid', the three rules and where it runs. The builder is one press further: the record's Edit, or Change hands and rules.
- **Log a high hand** is the Live floor's (RD-24): the record's button opens it there (`/?log=high-hand`), and only those who add Bonus by hand see it (0621).
- **Tonight's hits** come from `high_hand_tonight`, for staff who seat players or manage promotions: the whole night's count and Bonus, and the latest fifty. A record asks for its own promotion's, so an ended one never shows the hits of the one that runs now.
- **The simulation** replays last week's logged hits, each as confirmed, so it says what these terms would have paid for them.
