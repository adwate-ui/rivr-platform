# 0497: No audience of women

Date: 3 Nov 2026 · Owner's answer to a question in #829 · Supersedes the "women" audience of decisions 0472 and 0494

**Why.** The club records no gender, so a promotion for "Women members" matched nobody: it could be offered, approved and shown, and never give anything. The owner chose to drop it rather than start recording gender (personal data, erasure registry).

**What.**
- The audience is everyone, new members (first 30 days) or lapsed regulars: in `@rivr/contracts` `AUDIENCES`, `@rivr/domain` `isEligible` and `describePromotion`, the builder, the typed terms' check (`private.is_promotion_terms`) and the eligibility twin (`private.promo_is_eligible`), which now matches only the three it names. The member facts the engine and last week's replay weigh carry no `isWoman`, and the golden file's two women cases go.
- A version that still named women and has run or waits to (scheduled, live or paused) is ended: it never gave anything, and widened to everyone it would start to. A draft or one waiting for approval goes back to draft with its simulation cleared, so it runs only once simulated again and approved by a second person, and the promotion stays open to edits. Every such version's audience is then written as everyone, so the row meets the new check; an ended one's card words still say who it was for. Seeds and templates had none.
- Ladies' Night is a word-only card, and is retired with the other cards (decision 0498).
