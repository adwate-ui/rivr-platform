# 0400: A DigiLocker check that comes back is verified at once

Date: 3 Nov 2026. The owner, after their own check on staging waited for review: "If the DigiLocker verification is successful, KYC must be approved immediately." Supersedes, where they concern the name, decisions 0030 ("a name ... that does not match DigiLocker's ... waits for staff") and 0035 ("a DigiLocker record in another name"), and the PAN step after a DigiLocker that held no PAN (#4).

- **Why the owner's check waited.** DigiLocker returned their Aadhaar through Cashfree and the face match scored 75 (Cashfree's default threshold is 0.75), but the check also asked for the Aadhaar's name letter for letter as the member registered it (`isSameName`). Either half failing sent it to review and threw the identity away; which half is not stored. Cashfree's sandbox answers face checks by rote (every staging liveness is 98) while its DigiLocker returns real Aadhaar data, so the evidence points to the name, not proof of it.
- **Successful DigiLocker** is DigiLocker's Aadhaar, with a date of birth, and a selfie the vendor matches to the Aadhaar's photo. `record_kyc_digilocker` then keeps the identity and settles the check as `verified`, through `private.settle_kyc` like every route, so the verified bonus and `kyc.completed` follow. The registered name no longer counts, and a DigiLocker without a PAN no longer waits for one: the PAN step is only for a DigiLocker that failed. `isSameName` is gone; the identity key still reads the document's name.
- **Still stopped**, as legal and safety rules: under 18 is refused; a PAN a verified member holds is refused; a clash with another member's check holds it for a KYC reviewer. The PAN step used to find a member verified on a PAN alone, which keeps no Aadhaar details, so a check with no PAN now also clashes with such a check on the same date of birth. A check that would keep neither a PAN nor an identity key is never verified. Decision 0035's "the gate always has a PAN" no longer holds for these: the identity key stands in.
- **The face match stays.** A face that is not the selfie's is someone else's Aadhaar: the check waits for staff and keeps none of that identity. Cashfree decides the match at its default threshold.
- **Left open.** An erased member on a break whose check kept only a PAN, back with a DigiLocker that holds none, is not matched to the block. The registered name is no longer checked against the Aadhaar, yet the cage's payout-name check compares bank names with it (the cage is hidden for now). Checks already waiting stay as they are.
