# 0621. The high hand bonus: the rule

Issue #965 (RD-22), with the owner's answers of 15 Nov to #940 (a fixed list of hands) and #941 (a payout is apart from the daily limit). Decided while building:

- **The rule in words.** A promotion lists hands, each with a Bonus in whole 50s, and holds three rules. A hit is a hand, its rank where the hand has one, and each rule confirmed. It pays its listed hand's Bonus, whole, every time: no rounding, no window, no best hand. The only cap is the promotion's budget, whole or not at all; what is left is the budget less what was paid, never below nothing. The terms in force are the live version's at the moment the floor logs the hit.
- **The fixed list is five hands:** royal flush, straight flush, four of a kind, Aces full, any full house. Ranks are the hit's, not the list's: a straight flush's high card (king to five), which four of a kind, the three of a full house. Aces full is the one listed hand held to a rank, as the prototype draws it. A full house of three aces is paid as Aces full where the promotion lists it and as any full house otherwise. A longer list (four aces apart from other fours, say) is a question for the owner.
- **The list lives in `@rivr/contracts`** (`HIGH_HANDS`, `CARD_RANKS`), because the terms' schema needs it and contracts cannot import the domain; the rule (`highHandPayout`, `ranksOf`) is in `@rivr/domain/promotions`, its SQL twin `private.high_hand_payout` proved on `promotionRewards.json` (operations `grant`, `paid`, `limits`, `left`).
- **Rules.** Both hole cards and shown at showdown are each held or not; the least players dealt in is 2 to 10 and is always confirmed. The floor confirms each rule the promotion holds; confirmed is true and nothing else; an unconfirmed rule is refused by name, the first in order.
- **It is a promotion's grant.** `log_high_hand` gives through `give_promotion_version`, so the payout is a `promotion_grant` and one `bonus` ledger transaction, and the budget, the list's counts and the simulation count it as they count any reward. `high_hand_hit` keeps the hit itself. The Bonus is credited under the promotion's name.
- **Paid every time, so a budget is required.** The terms take no limit a member, a day or in grants, and nothing else bounds what the floor pays, so the schema and the database refuse a high hand with no budget (whole or monthly). The safest reading; a question for the owner if a club wants none.
- **One runs in a venue:** scheduled, live or paused. A unique index holds it; submitting and approving refuse first, in words (`RVHH1`).
- **Who logs:** those who add Bonus by hand (`bonus.give`: super admin, admin, floor), as the owner ruled for Bonus on 9 Nov; it pays Bonus. Issue #967 says "the same roles that seat players", which would add cashiers and hosts: open with the owner, and a one-line change. Everyone who sees the Live floor reads tonight's hits. Never for one's own member record, by login, phone or name, as Bonus by hand. The player must be seated at that table now; one who has left is refused, and the refusal says to log before the seat is ended.
- **Refusals** carry a code and, where there is more to say, a detail: `RVHHN` none live, `RVHHS` not seated there, `RVHHE` not for this table, player or moment, `RVHHR` with the rule's own name (`hand_not_listed`, `wrong_rank`, `both_hole_cards_not_confirmed`, `showdown_not_confirmed`, `players_not_confirmed`), `RVHHB` with what is left of the budget. The same request id again is the same hit; for another player, table or hand it is refused.
- **Told:** the audit log (`high_hand.pay`, with who logged it), `high_hand.paid` in the outbox, and `bonus.credited` as for any Bonus. No consumer is handed a practice venue's events (`claim_outbox_deliveries`).
