# 0563. One row of tabs, and a TV that reports in

Date: 9 Nov 2026. From the 180-day audit: F-028, F-083, F-089, F-125, F-207.

**One row of tabs.** Rewards and Settings stacked a title, pill tabs and underline tabs. Each now has one row, and the
tab is the address: `/rewards?tab=points`, `/settings?tab=tvs`. `?part=` is gone; those addresses only ever ran on
staging. Who may open a tab is the rail's list, so a role sees only its own. Hand over is on the Door alone.

**A tab's actions stand in the title row.** A screen with tabs gives its parts the end of its title row, so a create
button never floats on a row of its own. Tables setup keeps New stake and New table subtle in their own sections: the
screen has two equal parts and no single gold action.

**Export has one home.** Beside the title on a screen with one table, beside the search or a section's heading
otherwise, never alone on a row.

**The console's defaults live in the library.** Inside the console's frame a Button, ChoiceChips or Input that names no
look is the filled one, so no screen can drift back. A field with a prefix or suffix stays boxed, at the same height.

**A TV's report has its own table.** `device` and `tv_screen` are audited, so a report each minute on either would write
an audit row a minute for every TV. `tv_heartbeat` has no audit trigger, as the report is not a change anyone made.
To a purge it is a log, as `alert` is: a table a TV moves every minute cannot hold up the check for changes since the archive.
