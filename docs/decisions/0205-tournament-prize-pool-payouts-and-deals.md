# 0205: Tournament prize pool, payouts, deals and the finish

Date: 2026-10-26 · Task: #121, #149 (and #117's guarantee) · Question: #470 (answered)

- **Prize pool.** What the buys put in for prizes (prices less fees and bounties), or the guarantee when that is more; the club pays the difference as overlay from `venue.tournament_overlay` into the pool when the tournament finishes (`prizePool`, twin `private.tournament_prize_pool`). The finish books it, so no `tournament.finished` consumer does.
- **Payouts (owner, #470).** The floor sets each paid place's share of the pool for each tournament, in percent to two places, adding up to exactly 100%, none above the place before's; there is no default table, and a tournament does not finish without them. Once a player is out in the money the shares stay as they are. The places paid become as many as the shares. With fewer runs than paid places, the places that exist share the pool in the same proportions. Each prize rounds down to ₹50 chips, the chips left over to first (`payoutTable`, twin `private.tournament_payout_table`).
- **Deals.** With two to nine left, the floor finishes by ICM (Malmuth-Harville, chances to thirty decimal places rounded down, each value to the nearest paisa) or chip-chop (chips in proportion) over the prizes those players would play for. Each is sure of the smallest of those prizes; each amount rounds down to ₹50 chips, the rest to the biggest stack. The database works the deal itself from the stacks (`chipChop`, `icmDeal`, twins `private.tournament_chip_chop`, `private.tournament_icm`); the places follow the chips.
- **The finish.** Played out with one left (or none, when the last player was busted too: the last out takes first), or by a deal, the stacks no more than the chips bought and kept with it. Staff never finish a tournament they are still playing, nor pay a bounty to themselves. It is refused while an alternate waits (the floor refunds them first), which closes batch 2's gap: an alternate never seated would count as a run and push every place down. Every run keeps its place (`tournament_entry.place`); a finished tournament takes no buys, busts, moves or refunds and leaves the TVs and Home.
- **Paying.** Each run in the money is paid once, to the wallet, through `private.credit_wallet` (lane A's contracted twin of `private.debit_wallet`, built here at lane A's word: the venue accounts it comes from, and the TDS withheld into `venue.tds_payable`). TDS is decision 0068's `private.tds_for` on that prize alone, withheld while switched on and recorded as shadow while off, kept on `tournament_payout`. Prizes are paid at the finish, not as each player busts. Lane A's TDS reports read `tds_withholding`, which needs a cage transaction, so prize TDS is not in them yet (#477, lane A).
- **League and events.** Each member's best place earns League points (`private.award_league_points`); `tournament.finished` is emitted once.
