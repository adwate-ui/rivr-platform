# 0463: What the engine gives on a version

Date: 2026-11-03 · Console v3, phase 7, step 2 · Builds on decision 0438

**What.**
- **Events.** A version is weighed for its reward's own event:
  - a buy-in, its own chips being the cage's paise ÷ 100, none when paid from bonus;
  - a closed visit's time inside its schedule, at its stakes and tables, with the points earned in it;
  - a check-in, with its number among the visits opened this IST month;
  - a referral, for each side;
  - a sign-up, fired once, the first time a member's identity check is verified, keyed by the member.
  Graduation, a logged hand and a date window give no version anything. The graduate freeroll is now a tournament entry on the graduate's next visit.
- **Bonus chips** are credited as bonus at once (chips × 100 paise). They expire after 7 days, or 30 for a referral or a sign-up, as those templates did.
- **Points** from a multiplier are credited to the member at once. The old rule path only recorded them, and nothing credited them.
- **Spins.** A free-spin grant holds its spins, and the wheel takes them one at a time until the last. A bonus won counts in the promotion's budget, as decision 0244 has it.
- **Perks** (cab home, tournament entry, waitlist priority) are grants for the floor to fulfil. Nothing reads waitlist priority yet (follow-up).
- **Once per event.** An event already weighed for versions gives nothing more. A grant that loses a race to the unique period key is skipped, not raised, so a buy-in never fails over a promotion.
- **Simulation** runs in the browser on `promotion_version_replay`: last week's events for the reward, with members, visits and keys as numbers, never ids.
