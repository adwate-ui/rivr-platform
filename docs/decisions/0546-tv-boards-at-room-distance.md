# 0546: TV boards at room distance, and how a hidden name reads

Date: 9 Nov 2026 · From the 180-day audit of 8 Nov (I-46 to I-49).

**Sizes, measured at 1920 by 1080.** The title shares the wordmark's row, so the columns start at y=214 instead of y=555. List names are 72 px, a long one 56 with what is beside it under it, so it is cut only as a last resort; numbers and stakes are 48, in cream. The call-time board's names go from 36 px (decision 0293) to 48, as 72 does not fit four tables of three players beside the seats opening: the time is compact (`1h 52m`) and a player away reads `Away`, so a name is not cut.

**Rows come from the stage.** A column shows what its height holds, counting the rows with a status under the name, and the rest go to `+N more`. Spin and League are top-N lists, with no more to tell of, so they say nothing.

**A hidden name** is left blank beside the seat or place number, replacing the stand-ins of decisions 0070, 0183 and 0163. A row with no number, a redemption or a spin, says `A member`: a blank there reads as a broken row.

**Tables with no list fold** (F-140). A table has a column only while someone waits for it, and the open ones with none are named on one line at the foot. This narrows decision 0467, where every open table was a column. With nobody waiting anywhere, the board says only that.

**A TV on a hidden board moves** to the waitlist, once, by migration (F-136). `tv_showing` is unchanged: reading the waitlist from a hidden mode would hand it to the stream overlay, a device paired on the tournament clock (decision 0256).

**Left out.** A side zone for the waitlist during a rotation (decision 0163). The social wall's photo (decision 0267).
