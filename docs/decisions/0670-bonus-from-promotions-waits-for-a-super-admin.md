# 0670 Bonus from promotions waits for a super admin

Date: 16 Nov 2026. The owner, 15 Nov: the Bonus a promotion calculates goes to super admins for approval, and reaches the player only once approved; the high hand is paid at once (the floor's log is its check).

- **Waiting, not paid.** Every Bonus the engine gives (sign-up, referral, check-in, a seat bonus and its tiers, a play-time step, an older rule's Bonus) is a `promotion_grant` with status `waiting` and the event it was calculated for (`event`): no ledger lines, no `bonus_grant`, no `bonus.credited`, so nothing reaches the member's balance, app, push or WhatsApp. Perks are given at once as before.
- **Outside it.** The high hand's grant (trigger `high_hand`, PR 3) is paid at once. Bonus by hand (`give_bonus_chips`) is no promotion's and keeps its own limits.
- **Approve** (`approve_promotion_bonus`, super admins alone) posts each exactly as the grant posted it: `credit_bonus` with the same amount, the promotion's name and the key `promotion:<grant>`, so the ledger has the same two lines and the push says "Bonus added". Its end counts from the approval. **Refuse** (`refuse_promotion_bonus`) needs a reason, posts nothing and tells the member nothing.
- One `promotion_bonus_decision` a request: who, how many, the total, the reason; the grants point at it. The same request id is answered with its first try; a Bonus another super admin decided is left as decided. Never one's own record (`RVOWN`). One Activity line a decision.
- **Limits.** A waiting Bonus counts toward its promotion's per-member limit and budget as one given, so the engine never gives it twice while it waits. A refused one counts toward nothing: the tally leaves it out, and the period's unique key holds only grants not refused, so the member's next event may take its slot. The refused event itself keeps its key and is never weighed again; a refused play-time step stays settled for its visit.
- **Where.** A tab of Promotions, "Bonus to approve" (the prototype has no approval queue), a page at a time on the server (`list_spec` `bonus_waiting`). The rail counts it beside promotions to approve for super admins, and it chimes (`URGENT_QUEUES`): the member waits for it at the table.
- **Reports.** The Bonus report and the member's Bonus history read `bonus_grant`, so a Bonus counts when approved. The member record's promotions and the MIS list what was given; the promotions list counts a waiting Bonus in its week and budget, as the limits do.
- Nothing waits at deploy: every grant made before is `given`.
