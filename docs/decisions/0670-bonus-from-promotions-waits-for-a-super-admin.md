# 0670 Bonus from promotions waits for a super admin

Date: 16 Nov 2026. The owner, 15 Nov: the Bonus a promotion calculates goes to super admins for approval, and reaches the player only once approved; the high hand is paid at once (the floor's log is its check).

- **Waiting, not paid.** Every Bonus the engine gives (sign-up, referral, check-in, a seat bonus and its tiers, a play-time step, an older rule's Bonus) is a `promotion_grant` with status `waiting` and the event it was calculated for (`event`): no ledger lines, no `bonus_grant`, no `bonus.credited`, so nothing reaches the member's balance, app, push or WhatsApp. Perks are given at once as before.
- **Outside it.** The high hand's grant (trigger `high_hand`) is paid at once. Bonus by hand (`give_bonus_chips`) and hourly rakeback (`give_rakeback`) are no promotion's: they keep their own limits and pay at once.
- **Approve** (`approve_promotion_bonus`, super admins alone) posts each exactly as the grant posted it: `credit_bonus` with the same amount, the promotion's name and the key `promotion:<grant>`, so the ledger has the same two lines and the push says "Bonus added". Its end counts from the approval. A frozen member's is left waiting and counted (`left_waiting`); the rest are approved. Ticked or all shown, the console asks first. **Refuse** (`refuse_promotion_bonus`) needs a reason, posts nothing and tells the member nothing. Erasing a member refuses theirs ("Member erased").
- One `promotion_bonus_decision` a request: who, how many, the total, the reason; the grants point at it. The same request id is answered with its first try; a Bonus another super admin decided is left as decided. Never one's own record (`RVOWN`). One Activity line a decision.
- **Limits.** A waiting Bonus counts toward its promotion's per-member limit, budget and tiers as one given, so the engine never gives it twice while it waits. A refused one counts toward none of them (`periodGiven` and the rest leave it out). It keeps its period slot, and the member's next grant in the period takes the slot after every grant there, refused ones too (`periodSlots`), so a refusal that is not the latest never blocks a later grant. The refused event keeps its key and is never weighed again; a refused play-time step stays settled for its visit.
- **Where.** A tab of Promotions, "To approve" (the prototype has no approval queue), a page at a time on the server (`list_spec` `bonus_waiting`). The rail counts it beside promotions to approve for super admins, and it chimes (`URGENT_QUEUES`): the member waits for it at the table.
- **Reports and reads.** The Bonus report and the member's Bonus history read `bonus_grant`, so a Bonus counts when approved; the MIS counts it in the month it was approved. The member record's promotions and the list's Given this week count what was given; its budget used counts a waiting Bonus, as the limits do. Promotion managers read given grants; what waits or was refused, super admins alone.
- Nothing waits at deploy: every grant made before is `given`.
