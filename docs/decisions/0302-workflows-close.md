# 0302: Every workflow closes

Date: 3 Nov 2026. The third of the workflow PRs from the workflow audit of 2 Nov ("things like requesting top ups, player seating, F&B ordering are not completed properly").

- **Top-ups.** An open top-up closes when its seat ends, whichever way it ends (the floor's Leave, the member's, a cash-out): its outcome is `left`, at the time the seat ended, by a trigger on `seat_session`. Those left open before were closed once. A request already followed the member to another seat, as a move keeps the same seat session.
- **Food and drink.** Staff who serve void an order not yet delivered, with a reason of 3 to 300 characters that the member sees (the form says so; staff say "Void", the member reads "Cancelled"); a void order is neither accepted, delivered, counted on the rail nor printed at the bar, and is listed apart, with why. An erased member's audit rows lose the reason, as they lose other staff notes. Orders carry no money in RIVR (the bar takes payment), so a void moves nothing. Staff who serve and read members (the floor) may order for a seat (`console_fnb_order`); Service, which reads no members (#640), serves and orders nothing, so it never learns who sits where. A comp stays for staff who run F&B. Each order shows when it was placed.
- **Seating.** A seat held for a waitlist call names its place (`held_seats.entry_id`), and the live floor seats the member there with one Seat, as the waitlist does.
- **The leaving list.** Once a leaving player's call time is done (or they have none), the floor frees their seat from the list with Leave.
- **Redemptions.** A cancelled redemption stays on the member's list, "Cancelled: why", where it used to vanish; the reason becomes the member's in an export too, and the cancel form says the member sees it. Reasons written before were staff notes: the club has no real members before launch, so none is shown that a member was not meant to read.
- **Spin wheel.** The screen asks `promotions.manage`, as its database functions always did; it had asked `floor.manage`, so only admins could spin.
- **Not done, and why.** The member cancelling a top-up or an order: the approved player redesign has neither. Undelivered orders closing at night's end: they leave every list at 06:00 already. Waiting spins lapsing: a question for the owner. The floor marking tournament interest: the owner's role list of 29 Oct keeps tournaments off the floor's console.
