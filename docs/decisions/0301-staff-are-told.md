# 0301: Staff are told

Date: 3 Nov 2026. The first of the three workflow PRs from the workflow audit of 2 Nov ("Ensure that right people are notified and take actions"); the owner chose a chime and a tab count, and in-person checks by hosts and the floor.

- **A count beside every queue.** `staff_queue_counts` gives, for the staff member's venue, how many wait in each queue they act on, keyed by the rail entry: join requests (Waitlist), top-ups, food and drink orders not yet delivered, the leaving list (Live floor), checks to review, redemptions to hand over, feedback tickets, promotions to approve (approvers only), the host queue's open calls and tournament interest not yet registered. Each count reads the queue's own list function, so it never differs from the screen it leads to, and a role that cannot see a queue gets no count for it. The console reads it every ten seconds on every screen.
- **Red where minutes matter.** Top-ups, join requests, food orders and the leaving list are counted in red; the rest in gold. Their sum leads the tab's title, `(3) Top-ups · RIVR`, and when any of them grows from one read to the next the console plays a short chime made in the browser (no sound file) and tells a screen reader how many urgent items wait (`UrgentChime`). A browser plays sound only once the page has been clicked or typed in since it loaded; until then the count and the announcement say it alone.
- **The owner's alerts.** A visit rated 1 or 2, alone or in any part of a detailed form (`feedback.low_rating`); an in-person check whose PAN recheck failed, which stops the member being seated (`kyc.recheck_failed`); and a break that starts while the member is seated (`member.break_while_seated`). Triggers on the rows raise them, so every path that writes the row is covered. A member's poor forms alert once a night, and a break that lengthens one already running alerts no more, so no member can flood the owner's WhatsApp. Like every alert, the WhatsApp words carry no personal data, and the details name the feedback, check or break, never the member, as investors read alerts too. Three kinds raised before without words now have them (`aml.settings_changed`, `venue.purged`, `tournaments.ticket_refund_failed`).
- **Checks in person.** `kyc.in_person` is its own permission, held by super admins, admins, hosts and the floor: members are sent to the desk, so the desk checks them. Reviewing the app's checks stays `kyc.review`; a host or the floor opens KYC review to the check in person alone. As more staff now verify at the desk, a check in person earns the verification bonus (decision 0298) only once its PAN recheck matches, a minute or so later, or once a KYC reviewer approves it after a recheck that differed, so a PAN that merely looks right earns no points. A member or venue frozen at that moment gets no bonus, so paying it never undoes the verification or the recheck.
