# 0676 DigiLocker on Zoop: documents by webhook, held until the member is back

Date: 16 Nov 2026. Task #1023. Built from Zoop's DigiLocker documentation (sent by the owner on 16 Nov) and one test-host run, captured on staging as structure only (no values).

- **Hand-off.** `startDigiLocker` asks Zoop for the Aadhaar (`ADHAR`) and the PAN (`PANCR`), with the documents to go to the `kyc-digilocker` function on the same project (`response_url`) and the member back to the player's `/kyc/return` by a plain GET (`redirect_method: GET`: the static app takes no POST, Zoop's default). The member goes to `sdk_url?mode=REDIRECT`, on Zoop's gateway only (`*.zoop.plus`, `*.zoop.one`); Zoop's session lasts 30 minutes.
- **Delivery.** Zoop posts the documents to `kyc-digilocker` (public, `verify_jwt = false`) with the session's key in `webhook-security-key` (and `webhook_security_key`). `record_kyc_started` keeps only the key's SHA-256. The webhook finds the session by the header's key alone (`kyc_digilocker_session`) and reads the body only then, 2 MB at most; a session takes one delivery, while its check still waits on it and the venue still takes checks (`assert_writable`: KYC sign-up not stopped, nothing frozen; a session that may not is dropped).
- **What is kept.** The adapter reads the Aadhaar XML (`KycRes/UidData`: the masked `uid`, `Poi` name and date of birth, `Poa`, the `Pht` photo) and the PAN record (`PAN@num`). Only those fields and the photo, as a `data:` URL, wait in `private.kyc_digilocker_delivery`: never the XML or the PDFs. A delivery that cannot be read, or names another session, is kept as a refusal, so the member is not left waiting.
- **Trust.** The XML's signature is not checked: the per-session key, which Zoop gives only to RIVR's server, and Zoop's TLS are the trust. Were the key ever seen by the member's browser, a made-up identity could be posted; a matching DigiLocker check still waits for staff while there is no liveness (decision 0675). Checking the signature is a follow-up.
- **Return.** `complete` takes the delivery. Until it comes the function answers `waiting` and the app asks again every 3 seconds for 2 minutes, then offers Try again. Zoop is asked where the session stands at most every 30 seconds for each member (`may_ask_kyc_digilocker_status`), so no member can make RIVR call Zoop without limit. A refusal, a session Zoop failed, or nothing 35 minutes after the start sends the member to the PAN step; an outage, or Zoop refusing RIVR's account (decision 0674), is asked again. The decision is `deliveryStep` in `@rivr/adapters`. Asking where it stands is no paid call; the face match is. A member who reopens the app at the DigiLocker step first asks `complete` once, so documents already in are taken rather than dropped by a new hand-off.
- **No full date of birth.** An Aadhaar with the year of birth alone is kept with none, so the check waits for staff for that reason.
- **Clean-up.** The delivery goes as soon as the check leaves the session (recorded either way, a new hand-off, reopened, erased, deleted: a trigger on `kyc_record`); an hourly job (`kyc-digilocker-deliveries-forget`) removes any left after two hours.
- **Consent page.** DigiLocker's own page showed both documents unticked, so the step now says to tick Aadhaar and PAN; whether Zoop can tick them is Zoop's to answer.
- **Before `kyc.at_the_club` goes off on production:** the whole check walked on staging on a phone, and Zoop's live keys set.
