# 0250: Tournament satellites and their tickets

Date: 2026-10-28 · Task: #117 · Question: #489 (answered "as recommended"), #517 (TDS on a ticket, answered)

- **A satellite** is a tournament the floor points at a target event at the same venue (`set_tournament_satellite`), before anyone has entered it or its clock has started, so no buy-in changes what it pays. The target has levels, has not finished and is open for registration. A satellite has no bounty, and its places' shares are not used.
- **Seats.** The prize pool (with any overlay its guarantee needs) divided by the target's buy-in, rounded down, never more than its runs (`satelliteSeats`, twin `private.tournament_satellite_seats`, golden `tournamentPayouts.json`). The floor finishes it played out, once no more players are left than the seats (one when there are none); there is no deal. With more than one left, the floor counts their chips, and the chips give their places, so League points, the winner and the leftover never follow the order players registered in.
- **What the finish pays.** Each run that finished in a seat gets a ticket worth the target's buy-in, and the seats' value moves from `venue.tournament_pool` to `venue.tournament_tickets` (players' money). What is left is paid in cash to the next finisher, as a prize. When every run has a seat there is no next finisher, so the last seat is paid it: our reading of the owner's alternative, raised on #517.
- **A ticket** pays one entry into the target, its fee included, from the tickets held: from the app by the member, or at the cage by a cashier with no cash taken. It has no cash value and never moves to another member. A member who wins two uses one; the other lapses.
- **Lapse.** A ticket still held when the target's registration closes, when the target finishes, or a day after the target's start with its clock never started, is refunded to the wallet within the minute. An alternate who entered with a ticket and is refunded gets the ticket back. A held ticket blocks erasure, as an unfinished tournament does.
- **TDS.** The owner answered #517: a ticket carries no TDS, won, used or refunded; only prizes later won in the target are taxed. The leftover cash is taxed as any prize.
