# 0711 The floor's call time warns, a referrer from the console, visits added

Date: 25 Nov 2026. The owner's three requests of 25 Nov and their answers (one PR; #1132, #1133, #1134).

- **Call time warns.** Every console user who sets call time (`call_time.override`) may pick a time before the minimum playing time or the current end, or one gone, which ends the call time now at the whole minutes played. `add_seat_call_time` refuses it (`RVCTE`, `RVCTG`) until sent again with `p_confirmed`, so the server, not a polled snapshot, decides; the sheet asks with ConfirmQuestion. A day at most stays hard. One rule, `floorCallTimeUntil`, with its twin on `callTimeUntil.json`. Undo owes again a call time the sweep had marked done.
- **Referrer.** The code goes where sign-up keeps it (`sign_up_attribution.referral_code`), so the completion, the sweep, the funnel and the export read one fact. Changeable until paid, then locked. A new permission, `members.referrer` (floor, admins, super admins): no existing one fitted. My calls: no staff member's own record on either side; the practice venue refuses; no Remove.
- **Visits added.** Every add is a `visit_request` row (approved at once for a super admin, else waiting), so no visit reader needs a filter for waiting ones. An added visit is one seat at its table for the whole visit, with no call time owed, closed `by_hand`; no visit.opened or visit.closed, no check-in or seat Bonus. A new permission, `visits.add`. Approvals sit under Promotions beside Bonus approvals, with their own queue count and chime. Edit and the file stay super admin only.
- **Weighed on its own night.** A play-time step's event is at the time weighed, as a seat's is, and a promotion approved after that time pays nothing; the live sweep weighs at now, so a running visit is as before. This also corrects Edit, which weighed an edited visit on today's weekday, and the closes that stamp an earlier time than now (a stale visit closed at 06:00 or 11:10): a step still unpaid then is weighed on that close's night and period. A promotion ended since pays nothing.
- **No seat Bonus, nothing while promotions are stopped.** An added seat is marked weighed for the minute's seat Bonus sweep. A super admin's add, an approval and a file are refused while promotions are stopped, as nothing weighs a closed visit's Bonus or its referral later; the floor's add may still wait. Staff never shorten call time on their own seat, nor set a referral or add a visit on their own record (login, phone or name).
- **A file's date** is the club night, as `clubDayMoment` reads a time: a check-in after midnight is that night's. Excel date and time cells are read as Excel writes them. Tables are named as tables setup names them, in any case.
