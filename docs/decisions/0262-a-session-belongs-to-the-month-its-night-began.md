# 0262: A session belongs to the month its night began; League keeps its own rule

Date: 2026-10-28 · For #549 ("decide whether the two should agree").

**Decision.** They stay as they are.
- **Sessions** puts a visit, and now a tournament, in the club month its night began (`private.league_month(opened_at)`). A member reads their history by the night they remember: a night from 30 Nov 22:00 to 1 Dec 07:00 is November's.
- **League** keeps its own rule, unchanged. It is a points rule players see, and changing it is the owner's call, not this task's.

**So:** on that one night a year at a month's turn, the session's month and the League month can differ. Nothing about money changes, and each screen is consistent with itself.
