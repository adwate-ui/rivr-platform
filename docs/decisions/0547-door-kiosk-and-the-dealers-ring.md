# 0547: The door kiosk and the dealer's ring

Date: 9 Nov 2026 · From the 180-day audit of 8 Nov (I-50 to I-53).

**Until a device knows what it is, it is RIVR.** A member may be reading it at the door, so loading, failing and the offline start page no longer say Dealer tablet. A door kiosk stays RIVR after that too, as its name is one staff gave it.

**Offline with no hand held** is what it means here, never a count of nothing. Before the device is known, only that it is offline.

**A frozen member** reads the shared line, large then beneath: `Account paused.` / `Ask at the front desk.`

**The PIN field.** The pad's dots are the label of a hidden password field, so a tap on them focuses it and a password manager can fill it. The field comes first in the Tab order. The keys still write into it.

**A refusal stays until the next scan,** so whoever was refused can read why. A check-in or out still clears after six seconds.

**The ring** says one thing of a seat at most: leaving first, as the floor needs the seat, then call time in its last fifteen minutes, then away. Seat 1 is at the dealer's left of the tray, as on the felt. Only the tablet renders `SeatRing`: the console's live floor is a straight `TableTile` with no tray, so nothing changes there.
