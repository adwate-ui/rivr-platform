# 0298: Pre-registration: the verification bonus, notices and analytics

Date: 2 Nov 2026. The owner: "Till launch, players can signup > selfie > KYC > done. Add bonus X points to their wallet. Add a banner saying 'We're launching soon', text editable from backend. NOTICES feature for future notices. Setup GTAG console > meta pixel, GA." The answers: points usable at once, the amount set in Settings; one Google Tag Manager container (GTM-TQGH629H).

- **The flow stays as it is.** Sign-up, the selfie and DigiLocker already lead to Home; Home then carries the launch notice.
- **The bonus.** `points.verified_bonus` (Settings, Members and rewards) gives that many points, as earned points, once, when a member's identity check is verified, whichever way. It is the one automatic grant beside points by hand (#629). A 0 from 15 November 00:00 IST, set by the migration, ends it at launch; the club sets the number now. A member verified again after a recheck gets nothing more. Never at the practice venue. Members verified before the club sets the number get theirs by hand (Rewards, points by hand, #629).
- **Notices.** A `notice` table, written and ended in Settings → Notices by staff with `settings.manage`, read by every member of the club at the top of Home (`notices.view`, not gated by tier). The launch notice, "We're launching soon. RIVR opens on Sunday 15 November.", ends when the club opens. An end time in the form is left for later: a notice stays until ended.
- **Analytics.** Google Tag Manager loads only after a visitor allows "Analytics and ads" in the cookie banner: on the website (beside maps) and, new, in the player app, where Profile changes the choice later. GA4 and the Meta pixel are set up inside the container by the club, not in code. The choice is kept in the browser only. The privacy notice's cookies section says so, while it still reads as shipped. Staging loads the same container: filter staging's hostnames out in GA4.
- **What the container may run (security review).** The app keeps a member's session in the browser, so the page tells Tag Manager to block Custom HTML tags and custom JavaScript variables (`gtm.blocklist: customScripts`): nothing published in the container can read it. Put GA4 in with Google's own tag and the Meta pixel with its template from the Community Template Gallery, never as Custom HTML. Keep "automatic advanced matching" off in Meta's Events Manager, as the sign-in screen has a phone field. Give few people publish rights on the container, each with two-step sign-in. Tag Manager never loads on a draft's preview page, whose address is its key; turning analytics off clears its cookies and reloads at once.
