# 0640. Home's one card and one carousel

Redesign RD-20 (#963): the player's Home, Bonus and Profile. What the spec left open, and what was built.

- **The top slot's rule** is `homeTopSlot` in `@rivr/domain/notices`: nothing while a seat is called or taken; else the ID check while it is not done; else the notice in force that started last. The server still decides which notices are in force (`my_notices`, which now gives each one's start), so a phone with a wrong clock hides none. Decision 0424's two cards at once are gone from Home; the console may still put up as many as it likes.
- **Where the card shows.** Browsing, above the offers. On a list, under the place and Leave waitlist, then the offers, as the prototype's source orders them; the waitlist's own view is unchanged. The brief says neither card shows while a seat is called or taken; the prototype's source would still show the ID check then, and the brief wins.
- **The offers** are one carousel in `@rivr/ui` (`OfferCarousel`): one card at a time, on every five seconds as the prototype does, a dot each. A dot holds its slide; so does a swipe, or the focus moving into a card. Someone who asks their device for less motion gets no movement of its own. Dots are 44 px targets (the prototype's are 28), by the repository's rule. It shows while browsing, on a list and seated, as the prototype's renders do, and not while a seat is called. A card is one entry in a list of live offers, drawn by its kind (`TonightOffers.tsx`): a new kind is one more member of `LiveOffer`.
- **The header's circle stays on Home.** The renders hide the top row under a toast; the prototype's source has the wordmark and the Profile circle there.
- **Table rows** are today's (correction 13): the name with its stake, the minimum buy-in beneath, no counts.
- **No count reaches a member's phone.** The app drew none, but two reads Home makes every ten seconds still carried them. `waitlist_lengths` now gives a member each table's call time and no count of who waits, and `floor_open_seats` gives a member nothing; staff read both as before (migration `20261120300100`, test 481; tests 029, 091, 206 and 247 read their counts as the floor). `floor_taken_seats`, which no screen has called since the seat map left the join sheet, answers with no rows: taken seat numbers are a count of who is seated by another name. It is kept, empty, so an old build's call still succeeds; it and `useFloorTakenSeats` can be dropped once no such build is in use.
- **Refer a friend** promises a reward only while `referralPromise` (`@rivr/domain/referral`) says so, from the Bonus the live referral promotion gives; with none live the card on Bonus has no line about a reward and Home has no referral card.
- **The balance's line** follows `bonus.expiry_days` as in force, which `my_bonus` now returns: "Never expires." for none set or 0, else "Expires after N days." (`bonusLastsDays`, `@rivr/domain/bonus`).
- **Bonus's look**, from the UI check of PR 983: the balance large in cream under its name (`BalanceTile look="hero"`), each line of history with its day beneath and what came in gold (`DetailRow detail`). The test that said "never gold" now says gold.
- **Profile** hides the Bonus notification line; the member's choice and the push stay as they were.
- **Kept from today, though the renders differ:** the seated header's "playing 19 min" (the render has the minimum buy-in), "All tables" over the list (the render says "Tables" with filter chips, which correction 13 rules out), Copy link beside Share invite, Analytics and the phone's push switch on Profile.
