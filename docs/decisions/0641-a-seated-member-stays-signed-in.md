# 0641. A seated member stays signed in

Redesign RD-34 (#977): the owner, 15 Nov, said a seated member had to sign in again to see their call time. Production was read, read-only, on 15 Nov; this is what it said and what was built.

- **The server ended no session.** Production's and staging's Auth settings match the repository: an access token of 3,600 s, refresh rotation on with a 10 s reuse interval, no time-box, no inactivity timeout, not single-session. On the trial night 61 of 68 renewals returned 200, one after 35 hours idle, and three members seated for hours with locked phones (iPhone Safari, Android Chrome) renewed and carried on. "Locked for hours, then opened" works on production today.
- **How members did have to sign in again.** They signed in inside Instagram's or Facebook's in-app browser and later opened the app in Safari or Chrome, which is another browser's storage: 30 of 57 sessions since 13 Nov were made in an in-app browser. Logins the server no longer had after the wipe of 12 Nov. And Sign out, pressed. What to do about the in-app browser is the owner's to decide; nothing is built for it here.
- **What the client does.** The session is kept in `localStorage` under `rivr-session` and renewed by itself (`packages/db/src/client.ts`). A renewal that fails for want of a network keeps the stored member (`provider.tsx`); one Auth refuses removes the session and the frame sends the member to sign-in.
- **The one fault found in the client.** `getSession()` had no handler for failing outright, so the app stayed on loading for ever. It now goes on as the stored session's member, as with no network, or as nobody when none is stored (`provider.test.tsx`). In every app, not only the one that keeps a night: loading for ever helps no one.
- **The flow that guards it,** `e2e/flows/stays-signed-in.spec.ts`: a seated member's app opened with a session whose hour ran out five hours ago renews once and shows the call time; opened with no connection it shows the seat it kept, then Home once online; and a renewal whose reply never reached the phone, the old token presented again past the ten seconds, is answered by Auth and signs nobody out.
- **Not changed:** Auth's settings, CI, Sign out, and no screen for the in-app browser.
