# 0299: The floor and the waitlist, as the owner runs them

Date: 2 Nov 2026. The owner's list after the staging walks (super admin section).

- **Stakes.** "Straddle option in NEW Stakes" and "Game type is not needed when adding stakes - reserves future flexibility". A stake is its name, its blinds and any compulsory straddle (whole ₹50 chips above the big blind, `table_type.straddle_paise`, which the website already reads). The game is no longer asked: `table_type.game` may be empty, a stake made from now has none and its code comes from its blinds (`100_200`), and a stake made before keeps its game, shown in games history as before. Changing a stake sets or takes off its straddle too.
- **Assign, not Call.** "Waitlist - CALL to ASSIGN" and "Seat him automatically". Assign picks a table, a seat and a call time and seats the member at once (`assign_from_waitlist`: the call, whose seat-ready message still goes out, and the seating, in one transaction). A place the roll-on calls by itself, when someone ahead leaves the list, still waits for Seat.
- **Hold place and Remove.** The owner asked the difference. Skip, renamed Hold place, keeps a member's place while they are away, for the hold time, and the next name gets any seat; Back brings them back. Remove takes them off the list. A line under the lists says so.
- **Seat by membership number.** The live floor's seat field takes a mobile number or a membership number: ten digits, or +91 and ten, are a mobile; one to nine digits a membership number (`seat_member` takes either).
- **Call time when seated.** "180, 240, 300, custom." Assign and Seat both ask it: 3 h, 4 h, 5 h, or Custom in minutes (0 to 1,440). Less than the stake's own needs an admin's override, as `set_seat_call_time` already asks; a player changing tables keeps the call time they have.
- **The seated row.** It says since when the player plays and their call time left, or that none was given. Away, Move and Mark leaving are hidden (the owner: the floor gives more call time to a player who steps away, and assigns a player again from the waitlist to change tables); the tablet still marks away, and players still say they are leaving from the app, which fills the Leaving list and the TV's seats opening. Leave, Mark leaving and Leave early are one Leave: with no call time owed it asks "Yes, … leaves"; owed, an admin with the override gives a reason, which records the early departure and frees the seat in one step, or presses Leave owing to free the seat with no waiver, so the cage still holds their cash-out until an admin lets them go; staff without the override free the seat and the player is listed as left before call time, as before.
- **Top-ups on their own page.** "TOPUP as a separate page. SHOW RED MARKERS for pending top up requests. And a counter since requested. TAT is 60 seconds." Top-ups (`/top-ups`, the floor's) lists the requests waiting, oldest first, each with a clock counting up from the ask that turns into a red "Late" marker past 60 seconds (`TOP_UP_TARGET_SECONDS`, `@rivr/domain/topUps`), and Start, Done and Reset on its line; tonight's closed requests follow, with how long each took. The rail counts the waiting ones in red, read every five seconds, and a table with one shows a red Top-up marker on the room plan. The live floor's panel and the row's top-up buttons are gone: one place to handle them.
