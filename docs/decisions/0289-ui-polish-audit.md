# 0289: The UI polish audit and the owner's answers

Date: 2 Nov 2026. Report: `docs/design/ui-polish-audit.md`. Work in six batches (B1 to B6), tracked in the UI Polish Tracker.

- **The audit:** a live walkthrough of every app, KYC on Cashfree's sandbox, a static audit of `@rivr/ui` and the copy, and an asset review.
- **Hidden features lead to Tonight.** A screen hidden for the member's tier (Learn, Events, and Academy through Learn) sends its address to Tonight. It never shows an empty screen, as decision 0284 meant.
- **Copy:** the owner found the app's copy over-explained ("reads like AI slop"). Batch B3 tightens the voice in `.claude/rules/copy.md`:
  - the fact, then stop;
  - labels of one to three words;
  - status lines of eight words or fewer;
  - errors in one sentence ending in what to do;
  - no intro or hint under a heading unless a field cannot be filled without it.
- **The owner's answers:**
  - **Table art.** Use licensed assets from online. No free, real, top-down table photo exists, so the seat map's table is drawn in `@rivr/ui` from the tokens. A paid overhead photo stays the owner's option.
  - **Placeholders until the club's own shoot.** Licensed stock photos (Pexels and Unsplash licences) stand in, marked as placeholders. This suspends `ui.md`'s "never stock" for the website until the shoot. The website's placeholder phone number stays until the owner gives the real one.
  - **League points** are added by hand or by CSV.
  - **Type:** keep the investor deck's faces, with Bodoni Moda's optical sizes and a matching ₹. Licensed faces only with the brand's designer.
- **KYC under review:** the app stays locked to the review screen (identity rule, safest reading). Whether a member may read Tonight while under review goes to the owner as a question.
- **KYC locally:** Cashfree refuses a DigiLocker `redirect_url` that is not https.
  - To run it on a local stack, put the sandbox keys in the gitignored `supabase/functions/.env` and set `PLAYER_APP_URL=https://127.0.0.1:<player port>`.
  - In the browser, map that address back to http, as the audit's Playwright run did.
  - Never commit the keys.
- **Narrower than the audit asked, on purpose:**
  - E-14: the rail keeps its pinned foot (sign-out stays in view, `ConsoleShell.test`). A hairline above the foot and the item it cuts off are the cue that the list goes on; no fade, as the visual standard has no gradients.
  - K-05: `lang="en-IN"` is the right language tag for every app, but a date field's order follows the browser's own locale, so it does not change mm/dd/yyyy on a phone set to US English.
  - K-04 moved out: the functions do not log to the console (`no-console`, a project rule), so a vendor refusal's trace belongs with the vendor-call row in the database, a schema change of its own (follow-up issue).
- **Findings corrected on reading the code:**
  - E-03 and E-09 were the local stack answering within the 80 ms between taps; busy states hold on a real network.
  - E-05: a revoked tablet leaves at its next 30-second check.
  - E-13: an empty number never leaves the field.
  - E-10: Leave's refusals are reported; only its busy state was missing.
- **B4, the member and website pass:**
  - **The display ₹** is Noto Serif Display's, subset to the one glyph (1 KB, OFL) and served inside the Bodoni Moda family with the same style and weights; Tiro Devanagari leaves the stack. Bodoni Moda is now the variable face, so its optical size follows the font size and its weights from 400 to 900 exist (UI-16).
  - **The share card** carries the display face: it reads the face's file from the page's own stylesheet and embeds it, since an SVG drawn as an image cannot reach the page's fonts.
  - **Website stills** are offered as AVIF and WebP at 640, 1280, 1920 and 2560 px, made by `packages/ui/scripts/media.mjs` before every build and Storybook run. A still is never enlarged; today's 480 px placeholders stay as they are until B6 replaces them. `sharp` makes the files (and turns the app's picture into WebP): it is the encoder Astro already installs, and nothing in the repository encoded images before.
  - **On a phone the member app leads the header's links.** Beside the action it pushed the header to three rows at 375 px.
  - **The menu before a seat** lists what is on it, read only, under Food, Soft drinks and Bar.
  - **The rewards filter's fade** is a scroll cue, not decoration, as the gallery's caption scrim is for legibility: it says there is more to the right. E-14 kept its hairline because the rail's pinned foot already says so.
  - **The website's old claims** (every hand logged, points for every hour, rake back as points, League points by the hour or the finish, every night's money on record, the jackpot in the app picture) give way to neutral lines, or "Details to follow.", until the club writes its own in the Website editor, each only while it reads as first written. How League points are earned now is a question for the owner (#710).
  - **The Withdrawals notification switch** still shows while Balance is hidden: hiding its feature for every tier needs `private.bootstrap_venue` redefined, so it is a follow-up (#709).
- **B5, the console, tablet and TV pass:**
  - **Waitlist actions** show their verb; the name is on the row beside them and in each one's accessible name. Back's name is "Back: Kabir Sethi", so it starts with its label as the others do.
  - **The live floor** shows who still owes call time and who asks for a top-up where Hands and Per hour stood, and no pace on the cards: hands are no longer counted at the table (#630).
  - **A save that is not ready says why on press** (UI-19), on tables setup, rather than a greyed button that says nothing. Other disabled buttons are left as they are, screen by screen.
  - **The jackpot and buy-in bonus templates** are left out of the console's templates (Q8, no answer, the recommendation taken): no console runs jackpots or buy-ins since 0284.
  - **Staff** is one row per person, Change opening the role, job title and login in place; a reload closes it.
  - **The seat ring** fits a tablet in either orientation and keeps its labels off the felt. On the console's cards its type is larger, about 13 px drawn, but the cards stay two to a row: the table art of B6 (AS-08) is where the card's ring is drawn again.
  - **Found and left:** a seated player's row on the live floor crams its actions at 1440 px (#712), outside the audit.
  - **League points by hand or CSV (L-01)** is a schema change and waits for the owner's yes on its plan.
