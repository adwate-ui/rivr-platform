# 0708 Call time set to a time, TVs by board and table, the floor in Members

Date: 22 Nov 2026. The owner's five requests of 22 Nov and their answers (one PR).

- **Call time to a time.** The member's Set and Extend, and the floor's, take a time on the clock in the device's own picker; the lengths and their two settings (`call_time.add_choices`, `call_time.extend_choices`) go. The call time becomes the seconds played plus those to the time, in minutes rounded up. Until set, the time must fall after the minimum playing time the seat started with; once set, after the current end; a day at most. One rule, `callTimeUntil` in `@rivr/domain/callTime`, with its SQL twin proved on `callTimeUntil.json`; `my_seat` and `floor_seats` carry `call_time_after_at`, the time a pick must be later than, which the apps show as "After 23:40." and check before sending.
- **Which day a time is.** A time picked is the one the clock shows nearest now, within twelve hours either side: 01:30 picked at 23:50 is tonight's, and 22:00 picked at 22:44 has gone by and is refused. A slip of AM for PM cannot ask for most of a day.
- **The request row** keeps the time picked (`call_time_request.until_at`) for a retry with the same request id, and `minutes` as the length from now to it.
- **Tablemates.** `my_tablemates` gives the others at the member's table: seat, first name (none for a member who keeps their name off the TVs) and until when, or not set, away or done. Only for a member with `call_time.view`.
- **Minimum playing time** names the seating's field and the stake's default; "Call time" stays elsewhere, Settings' per-stake rule among them.
- **TVs.** Pairing picks the main board and what it turns to, as the TV's row does, and the tables its call time board shows (`tv_screen.tables`, none for every table; also on the row). Window-only pairing goes with its switch, button, functions and columns: every TV not yet paired gets a code at once, within the existing limits on how often.
- **The floor in Members.** Staff with `members.phone` (super admins, admins and the floor) open Members and see every part to view: the mobile, the selfie and the desk's photo, the tier and break, and the account's mobile and feature overrides. Editing stays with the permissions that had it; ID documents, DOB and address with super admins. An export carries the mobiles for super admins only, as before: view, not bulk copy.
