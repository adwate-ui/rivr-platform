# Lane A: money

Ledger rules, cage, wallet, withdrawals, markers and credit, TDS, GST, treasury and the end-of-day close, expenses and payment accounts, Tally, statements and the shareholding register, the owner dashboard and alerting, backups and restore, exports and purge, the kill-switch control, AML, staff rosters, the console Tiers screen, rakeback and accommodation rewards. Money paths get line-by-line review and golden tests (`.claude/rules/money.md`).

## Tables owned

| Table | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `cage_transaction` | Buy-ins and cash-outs at the cage: method (cash, UPI with UTR, wallet, marker), amount, member, ledger transaction, idempotency key; a second approver, where one is needed, is a `second_approval` |
| `buy_in_request` | A buy-in the dealer tablet or the floor sends to the cage queue (lane B inserts through `public.request_buy_in`) |
| `second_approval` | Dual control (#20, decision 0063): a second approver's approval of a cage write at or above `cage.second_approver_threshold`, by subject (a withdrawal, #21) and amount, each approver once, with who approved and when; never changed; seen by the cage and its approvers. The one place a second approver is recorded |
| `bonus_grant` | Each credit to a member's bonus (#17): amount, what is left, its end and reason, its ledger transaction, and when it expired with the entry reversing what was left; seen by the member and by staff who transact at the cage or read the ledger |
| `deposit_request` | A member's UPI deposit (#29, decision 0064): amount (whole rupees), UTR (given once, ever) and the verified UPI account it came from; `waiting`, `received` (its ledger transaction, bank to wallet) or `not_found`; who decided and when. Seen by the member and the venue's cage and its approvers. A trigger on every table holding a UTR (buy-ins, Academy payments and turn-downs, withdrawals, deposits) keeps one UTR to one payment (`RVUTR`) |
| `payout_account` | A member's UPI addresses and bank accounts for withdrawals (#19): the bank's name, waiting, verified or turned down (with the reason and the cashier who decided), removed; at most three in use. Seen by the member and the venue's cage staff; the audit log keeps how it moved, never its number, IFSC, holder or address. Usable for a withdrawal only while verified and not removed |
| `withdrawal_request` | Withdrawals from the app (#21): amount (whole rupees), the verified payout account, `waiting`, `sent` with its UTR (once per venue) or `declined` with the reason; the hold from the wallet (into `venue.withdrawals_pending`, players' money) and its payment to the bank or return; who decided and when. | `credit_line`, `marker` | Limits per member, markers issued against them, repayment, ageing, the room exposure cap. `credit_line` (#73, decision 0058): the limit in whole ₹50 chips and any raise waiting for a second super admin, with who set, proposed and approved it; seen by staff who set limits, issue markers or read the ledger |
| `tds_withholding` | TDS on each cash-out (#22, decision 0068) and each tournament prize or bounty (#477, decision 0233; exactly one of `cage_transaction_id` and `tournament_payout_id`, written by a trigger on `tournament_payout`): the parameters in force, the winnings (a cash-out's period, or the prize and bounty alone, basis `payment`) and what the period already recorded, what was due and what this payment bore, withheld or shadow. Append-only; the member sees their own, ledger readers the venue's |
| `invoice_series`, `invoice_counter`, `invoice` | GST tax invoices per venue, numbered per series and financial year without gaps (`RIVR/S26/41`), never changed once issued. Every venue has series S for the club's own supply. Issued only through `private.issue_invoice(venue, series, source, inclusive paise, rate, description, member or buyer and GSTIN, dated at)`; a tax invoice stores its series' SAC code, the place of supply and the authorised signatory from venue configuration, and is refused without the SAC code or when the place of supply's state code names no state (#423); once per source (a repeat with other details is refused), with GST taken out of the amount (`private.gst_included`, twin of `@rivr/domain` `gstIncluded`) as CGST and SGST, or, for a buyer whose GSTIN is from another state than the venue's, as IGST in `igst_paise` (`private.igst_included`, twin of `igstIncluded`; #380, decision 0102). An invoice carries one kind or the other, and taxable plus CGST, SGST and IGST is its total. The practice venue's are never tax invoices. Members read their own; `ledger.read` staff their venue's (#23, decision 0071) |
| `cage_shift`, `chip_inventory_count`, `chip_inventory_line` | The cage's shift (#24, decision 0066): one open at a time per venue, opened with the float counted into the drawer; its counts of the chips in the cage, each line how many of one of the venue's denominations. Ledger readers see them; only the functions below write |
| `eod_close` | A cage shift's close (#45, decision 0067): the cash, chips, UPI and bank receipts and markers counted, what the ledger expected, whether any differs (an anomaly), and an anomaly's reason with who gave it. Ledger readers see it; only the functions below write |
| `expense_category`, `expense`, `payment_account`, `account_transfer` | Expenses with approver and attachments; cash drawer, UPI handles and bank accounts as ledger accounts (#94, #95, decision 0167). `payment_account`: the drawer and "Bank and UPI" every venue has, and the UPI handles and bank accounts treasury managers add, each its own asset account; ledger readers see them. `account_transfer`: money moved between two, with the ledger transaction and who moved it. `expense_category`: a kind of spending with its own expense account. `expense`: requested (with an optional receipt in the private `expense-receipts` bucket at `<venue>/<expense>.<jpg|png|webp>`), then approved and posted or turned down with a reason, by a treasury manager other than who asked; the cage and treasury managers see them. Only the functions below write |
| `tally_mapping`, `tally_export` | Ledger-to-Tally voucher mapping and the daily export files |
| `shareholder`, `shareholding`, `holder_statement` | Partners and investors, effective-dated holdings, monthly statements. `shareholder` and `shareholding` (#97, decision 0170): a partner or an investor, and a share in basis points from a day on, never above 100% on any day; super admins only, audited |
| `sar_entry`, `sar_entry_transaction` | The suspicious-activity register (#105, decision 0234): a pattern found in a member's cash buy-ins (`structuring_visit`, `structuring_day`), the buy-ins it is about, and its review (outcome, note, by whom). One open entry a member and kind; never deleted. `aml_flag` (source-of-funds flags) is not built yet |
| `staff_shift`, `staff_attendance`, `onboarding_item` | Rosters, shifts and attendance by job title; onboarding checklists linked to SOP acknowledgements (lane B owns SOPs) |
| `purge_run` | Every purge, audited. Every table export is recorded in the audit log (`export.table`: who, what, filters, format, rows; decision 0190), not a table of its own |
| `alert` | Alerts for the owner dashboard and the alert WhatsApp number |
| `accommodation_rate` | Hours of play per hotel night by stake, for accommodation rewards |
| `glaza_import_line`, `glaza_statement` | Monthly F&B CSV import from Glaza, matched to visits; statements |

A freeze, or a revoke, that would leave fewer than two super admins able to release a freeze is refused (`RVLCK`) (#27) |
| `private.raise_alert(venue, kind, detail)` | all | An owner-dashboard alert, and WhatsApp to the alert number |
| `private.day_rake(venue, day)`, `private.day_tournament_fees(venue, day)`, `private.issue_day_rake_invoice(venue, day)` | A (the daily job) | What `venue.rake` and `venue.tournament_fees` were credited in a business day (06:00 to 06:00 IST), and its one consolidated invoice for both in series S to unregistered customers, source `rake:YYYY-MM-DD`, dated 06:00 IST when the day began (or when issued, if after the 11th of the next month, #471) and at the `gst.config` rate in force then; null for a day without rake or fees or while GST is on a table fee (#422, #469, decision 0194). The cron job `daily-rake-invoice` runs `private.issue_rake_invoices()` at 07:00 IST for the last 35 ended days at every venue, so a day refused or missed is invoiced later; it raises `invoice.rake_refused` for a venue refused and `invoice.rake_negative` for a night whose reversals exceed its rake and fees (#379, decision 0106) |
