# Lane B: floor

Tables and table types, seating, the waitlist and its priority, call time, the dealer tablet (hand entry, rake, jackpot drop, offline queue, down and drop-box), pot data, jackpots with their sub-pools, high hands, TVs and their modes, tournaments, CCTV references, table-side food and drink, the practice venue's training use, certification quizzes, SOPs, POCs, incidents and the offline pack, dealer rotation and tip-pool export, and the final-table stream overlay. The tablet and the money it records get line-by-line review and golden tests.

## Tables owned

| Table | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `table_type` | Game, blinds, buy-in range, straddle; its `code` is the subject of the effective-dated settings `rake.config` (rate and cap) and `call_time.minutes`; the jackpot drop is the venue's `jackpot.drop` (#34) |
| `game_table` | The six tables: name, type, open or closed |
| `seat_session` | A member in a seat: start, end, table moves and breaks (drives call time and hours) |
| `waitlist_entry` | Per stake and game: joined at, priority reason, hold expiry, called, seated, skipped |
| `interest_list` | Interest in a game that is not running |
| `early_departure` | A cash-out or check-out before call time, with the admin override and reason |
| `dealer_down` | A dealer's shift at one table: on and off times |
| `down_count` | A down's drop-box count, what its hands logged and the variance; staff who read the ledger see it (#44) |
| `seat_session`, `seat_move` | A member's play from seating to leaving, inside a visit, across seat moves (each move kept); a table's must-move link (`game_table.must_move_to_table_id`) (#77) |
| `tablet_assignment` | Which dealer tablet (lane 0 `device`) is fixed to which table: one each way (#41) |
| `device_pairing` | A dealer tablet's six-digit pairing codes, by hash, for ten minutes; read and written only by the functions below (#41) |
| `hand` | Every hand: table, dealer, variant, pot, rake, cap applied, jackpot drop, high-hand claim, camera reference, idempotency key |
| `high_hand_claim` | High hands claimed on the tablet (one per hand: category, seat, rank), approved or rejected by the floor once and for all, and what was paid; read by staff who pay jackpots or read the ledger, and by the tablet that claimed. The club pays them in chips from `venue.high_hand_expense` under `high_hand.payouts` (#47, decision 0050) |
| `jackpot_event` | Bad-beat payouts and their reversing adjustments (#48 part 2). The pools themselves are the ledger accounts `venue.jackpot.main`, `.season` and `.freeroll`; their shares of the drop are the setting `jackpot.shares` and the payout rule `jackpot.payout` (decision 0048) |
| `tv_screen` | The display mode each TV shows (`tv_mode`), set when it is paired and changed on the console (#49) |
| `private.tv_pairing` | A TV's requests to be paired: the code it shows and the secret it asks with, by hash, for fifteen minutes; no venue until claimed, and no client role reads it (#49, decision 0049) |
| `tournament_template`, `blind_structure`, `tournament`, `tournament_level`, `tournament_entry`, `tournament_seat`, `payout_structure`, `tournament_deal` | Every tournament format, the server-side clock, entries and re-entries, alternates, seating and balancing, bust-outs, payouts, ICM and chip-chop |
| `camera`, `cctv_reference` | Camera ids and playback links on hands, cage transactions and incidents |
| `fnb_order` | Table-side orders, KOT printing, comp or paid, posted to the visit |
| `quiz`, `quiz_attempt` | Dealer and cashier certification, pass recorded on the staff record |
| `sop`, `sop_version`, `sop_ack`, `poc`, `incident`, `drill_schedule` | SOP library with acknowledgements by role, the escalation directory, the incident log, drills |

| `public.join_waitlist(table_type, idempotency_key)` | player app (C screens) | Joins the list for a table type (one game and stake) at the member's venue; refuses without KYC (`RVKYC`) or while frozen (`RVFRZ`, `RVMBR`), and on a break (`RVBRK`, #56); feature `waitlist.join`; emits `waitlist.joined`. | `public.add_to_waitlist(table_type, phone, idempotency_key)` | console | The floor (`waitlist.manage`) adds a member found by mobile number (`RVNOM` when none); same refusals as joining |
| `public.start_device_pairing(device)`, `public.assign_tablet(device, table)` | console | Staff with `devices.manage` start pairing a dealer tablet, or pair it again (a six-digit code for ten minutes, shown once), and fix a dealer tablet to one of the venue's tables, freeing the tablet that had it. Staff add a tablet and revoke one through lane 0's `device` table grants; a trigger records who added it, holds an enrolment during a freeze, and on revoking unlinks the login and frees the table for good (#41, decision 0045) |
| `public.claim_tv_pairing(venue, code, name, mode)`, `public.set_tv_mode(device, mode)` | console | Staff with `devices.manage` type in the code a TV shows, with its name and mode: the TV is enrolled as a device and signed in on its next ask; a wrong, used or expired code is `RVCOD`; a freeze is `RVFRZ`. Unpairing a TV is revoking its device (#49, decision 0049) |
| `public.tv_waitlist_board()`, `public.tv_jackpot()` | display | The signed-in TV's own venue only, the waitlist board only while the TV shows it: every stake with its open tables and its places in the floor's order, each player as a first name and initial; the main pool and tonight's best high hand. Nothing for anyone else. The database broadcasts `waitlist` and `jackpot` on the private Realtime topic `tv:<venue id>` when a board changes; only the venue's TVs may subscribe (#65, decision 0051) |
| `public.my_tv_screen()` | display | The signed-in TV: name and mode; nothing for anyone else or once it is unpaired |
| `public.claim_high_hand(hand, seat, category, rank)` | tablet | The dealer claims a high hand for a hand of the down open on the tablet, while the down's dealer may deal (`RVDWN`), at a seat the table has (`RVSEA`), with the rank of a four of a kind from A to 2; a category that pays nothing is refused (22023); a replay is the same claim, also when two land at once, and a different claim for the hand is 23505; refused while frozen or with promotions off; emits `high_hand.claimed` (#47) |
| `public.approve_high_hand(claim, expected_paise)`, `public.reject_high_hand(claim, reason)`, `public.high_hands(venue)` | console | Staff with `jackpots.approve`, other than the dealer of the hand (42501), approve a claim, paying the amount for its category in force when the hand was played, in chips at the club's cost (`RVSTL` if it is not the amount shown; `RVDUP` once decided; `RVKIL`, `RVFRZ`), emitting `high_hand.approved`; or reject it with a reason. `high_hands` lists the venue's claims, latest first, with what a waiting one pays now, for staff who approve jackpots or read the ledger (#47, decision 0050) |
| `public.my_device()` | tablet, display | The signed-in device: name, kind and the table it is fixed to; nothing for anyone else |
| Edge Function `device-pair` (`redeem_device_pairing(code, client)`, `start_tv_pairing(client)`, `check_tv_pairing(secret)`, `link_device_login`, service role only) | tablet, display | A device gets its own session signed out: a +999 phone login with a random password nobody keeps, which the token hook accepts only by that password and only while the device is paired. A tablet sends the code staff read out; five wrong codes from one address, or fifty in all, in ten minutes and redemptions wait (429), with an alert (#41, decision 0045). A TV asks for a code to show (`{tv: "start"}`: code, secret, expiry; thirty from one address, an IPv6 address by its /64, or two hundred in all in ten minutes, then 429), then asks with its secret (`{tv: "check", secret}`) until the code is claimed: `{waiting: true}`, then its session; again 30 seconds later only if that sign-in failed, and nothing 15 minutes after the claim (400 `expired`). JSON bodies only (415 otherwise) (#49, decision 0049) |
| `public.set_dealer_pin(staff, pin)`, `public.venue_dealers(venue)` | console | Staff with `staff.manage` who may grant the dealer's role (the owner and the general manager, not an admin) set a dealer's four-digit PIN, kept as a bcrypt hash in `staff_credential`, and list the dealers whose PIN they may set, with any lock (#41, decision 0046) |
| `public.tablet_dealers()`, `public.dealer_sign_in(staff, pin)`, `public.end_down(drop_box_paise)`, `public.current_down()` | tablet | On a paired tablet fixed to a table: the dealers with a PIN; sign-in starts the dealer's `dealer_down` there and ends the one before it and the dealer's own elsewhere (a wrong PIN returns `wrong_pin` with the tries left; five lock it for 15 minutes); End of down takes the drop-box count, keeps it in `down_count` and returns what the hands logged and the variance, raising `dealer.variance` beyond `dealer.variance_threshold` (a down with hands closed without a count raises `dealer.uncounted`) (#44); who is dealing now. Not while the venue is frozen (#41, decision 0046) |
| `public.my_waitlist()`, `public.waitlist_lengths()`, `public.waitlist_board(venue)` | player app, console | The member's own places with their number on each list (never gated, decision 0040); how many wait per table type (members with `floor.live_view`, and staff); each list in priority order with names and `priority_reason`, the tier that put a place ahead of join time (`waitlist.manage`) |
| `public.leave_waitlist(entry)` | player app, console | A member gives up their own place (even while frozen), or the floor removes anyone's (not while the venue is frozen). A called place that leaves rolls its seat to the next name |
| `public.call_from_waitlist(entry, table, seat)`, `public.seat_from_waitlist(entry)`, `public.step_away(entry, is_away)` | console, player app | The floor calls a waiting member, not frozen, to an open table of their game and a seat no other call holds (`RVHLD`), held for `waitlist.hold_minutes` (emits `waitlist.called`: member, table name, seat, hold until; WhatsApp through lane D's consumer, #62); seats a called member there while the hold lasts (`RVLAT`) and the table is open, which takes the place off every list (emits `seat.taken`); a member or the floor (Skip) steps a place away, keeping it for the same hold counted once per place (`RVAWY` when none is left, `RVLAT` back too late), and the floor's Skip on a called member ends the call and rolls the seat on. | `public.log_hand(pot, variant, played_at, idempotency_key)`, `public.tablet_rake_rules()`, `public.down_hands()` | tablet | The table is the tablet's own, during a down begun on that tablet by a dealer who may still log hands (`RVDWN` otherwise); a pot of whole ₹50 chips up to ₹1 crore; played within that down. ## Events

- Emits: `waitlist.joined`, `waitlist.called`, `seat.taken`, `call_time.completed`, `hand.logged`, `high_hand.claimed`, `high_hand.approved`, `jackpot.paid`, `jackpot.reversed` (a payout taken back: retract anything that announced it), `tournament.starting`, `tournament.finished`, `tournament.entered`, `fnb.ordered`, `incident.logged`.
- Consumes: `visit.opened` and `visit.closed` (lane C) for seat sessions; `promotion.published` (lane D) for the TV rotation.

## Uses from other lanes

`rake.config` (percent and cap by table type; the console's Rake screen schedules a change from a time and withdraws one not yet in force, #37), `jackpot.drop` (amount, minimum pot), `jackpot.shares` (season and freeroll, up to 50% each; the main pool takes the rest), `waitlist.priority_weights`, `waitlist.hold_minutes`, `high_hand.payouts`, `tournament.late_registration_level` (per template), `dealer.variance_threshold`, `call_time.minutes` (lane 0 key, values by table type).
